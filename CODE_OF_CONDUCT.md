# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The short version: treat people with respect, criticise the work rather than the person, and be
patient with people who have less context than you. Unacceptable behaviour can be reported to
`conduct@hubtask.eu` and will be handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant applies as published at the link above.

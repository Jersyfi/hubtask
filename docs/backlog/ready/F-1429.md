**(d)** Rule 2: the guard is an invariant of the workspace, not an access decision — it counts
holders and decides nothing about the actor; the step-up is the use case's, as for every other
privileged act. Rule 3: the new queries run in the tenant transaction. Rule 10: identifiers only.
Rule 11: contract first. P-05: *People* hides *Revoke* where it can know from what it reads; D5
names where only the sentence explains. Known traps: *a write on a refusal path is rolled back* —
the ceiling and the early guard are asked before the write transaction; *`Handler.Invoke` skips
validation* — tests call `Execute`; *a read-then-write without a lock* — **two writers**: two
owners each revoking the other's `OWNER` (or one revoking, one taking somebody out of an owners
group) both see two owners. Guarded by a per-workspace transaction-level advisory lock
(`LockOwners`, the pattern of `legal_hold:` and `audit_log:`) that every door ending a workspace
`OWNER` takes inside its write transaction before it reads the owners again; the second waits,
sees one owner, and is refused. Lock order: a group change takes the group's row (#1421), then the
owners' lock; a revocation takes only the owners' lock; a grant takes only the group's row. No
cycle. A grant of `OWNER` takes no owners' lock: missing a grant committed meanwhile can only refuse
wrongly, never let the last owner go. An erasure takes no owners' lock either (D6): a revocation
beside it may count the person being erased. The guard is asked once read-only before the step-up
(a proof is not burnt on a refused act) and again under the lock (the decision).

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 The rule: the workspace keeps at least one owner — a door that would end the last
  counting owner's `OWNER` on the workspace is refused with `409 memberships.last_owner` —
  decided by: worker, after #1429's proposal. Written in `domain-model.md` §3.2.
- [x] D2 Who counts: a person (`USER`) whose account is `ACTIVE` or `RESTRICTED` (restriction is not
  a lockout) and not deleted, holding `OWNER` at the workspace directly or through a group — decided
  by: worker. Rejected: counting `INVITED` (an invitation may never be redeemed, leaving nobody),
  service accounts (nobody signs in as one to administer the workspace).
- [x] D3 Refuse only a change that **ends** the last owner: where no counting owner existed before,
  nothing is refused — decided by: worker; a workspace without one (an import, a service account as
  the only holder) would otherwise freeze every owner-grant cleanup.
- [x] D4 Group joins count: a member change that takes out every owner but adds a counting person
  to a group holding workspace `OWNER` passes — decided by: worker; replacing the owners group's
  members is a normal handover.
- [x] D5 Where the screen cannot know, the refusal's sentence explains — decided by: worker. *People*
  hides *Revoke* on a direct workspace `OWNER` grant when no other workspace `OWNER` row exists (it
  reads those rows and the accounts' status); where a group holds `OWNER` or the other holder is an
  account the screen cannot judge (kind, an invitation), loading every group's members and every
  account's kind for one rare refusal is not worth it. *Groups* reads no workspace memberships, and
  which removal would leave nobody depends on the member chosen. P-05's "the screen says why" holds
  through the code's sentence.
- [x] D6 Out of scope: an erasure (Art. 17) of the last owner is not refused by this guard — an
  erasure is the controller's legal duty, and refusing it is not a product rule a worker adds —
  decided by: worker; reported as a finding.
- [x] D7 The step-up for groups (#1430): a member change (a changed list) or a deletion of a group
  holding `OWNER` at any scope demands the proof, as granting or revoking `OWNER` does at any
  scope — decided by: identity.md §16.3. The decision reads the roles the ceiling asked about; a
  role added after is the existing `groups.roles_changed`. The proof travels as on every other
  route, the optional `X-Hubtask-Step-Up` header (`components/parameters/StepUpToken`, additive),
  and as the descriptors' `step_up_token` input for MCP.
- [x] D8 No ADR — consequences of existing rules (§3.2, §16.3) — decided by: worker.
- [x] D9 Lock by an advisory lock rather than the tenant's row — decided by: worker; the pattern the
  legal holds and the audit chain use, and a tenant row lock would serialise unrelated writes.
- [x] D10 #1431 (an administrator renewing a managed account that holds `OWNER`) is **not** fixed
  here — decided by: worker, because the fix needs the owner. The ceiling #1431 proposes would leave
  a managed sole owner who forgot their password, with only administrators left, without a way back
  (P-16, `docs/vision/`), and it narrows UC-ID-20 check 5 ("an owner or administrator can issue a
  new start password"), a *How to check* the text states the other way. Both are on AGENTS.md's
  list. The review also found sibling doors (`RequestAddress` on another's managed account,
  `RenewInvitation` in link mode for an invited owner, a token for a service account holding
  `OWNER`); they are reported with the question rather than fixed piecemeal.

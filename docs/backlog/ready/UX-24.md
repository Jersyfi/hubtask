# UX-24 — What counts

**Task:** UX-24 · issue #1656 · `docs/backlog/milestone-UX.md`
**Checked against:** `origin/main` at `5f2562f02` (UX-09, #1668, merged) on 2026-10-21; UX-29
(#1678, draft) for the hidden predicate this task reuses
**Verdict:** ready

UX-09 stores the setting *What counts* (`counts`: `{figure, field_key}`, seven figures) on every
layer and answers it inside the effective structure of every container read; nothing computes a
figure and nothing shows one. This task decides **where a figure is computed** — on the server, in
a read-only transaction of its own after the container read, never from the replica (D1, D2, D9) —
defines each figure exactly (D3–D7), makes "a list never shows a percentage" a rule of the domain
(D5), keeps the container read fast and whole when a figure is slow or fails (D11, D16), and draws
the figure in the collection's head and its navigation row. It depends on UX-29 for the one
predicate that says which entries are hidden (D17); every step that does not need it is built
first. The flow figure is UX-26's (D8); the *What counts* control is built here and moves into
UX-10's *Structure* panel unchanged (D12).

Milestone § UX-24 on `main` (quoted): "*Depends on: UX-09.* · issue #1656 · **Use cases:** UC-WRK-29
(1, 2, 3) · The setting *What counts* and its figures in the collection head and the navigation:
open, field sum, due, rhythm, progress, flow, none. · **Acceptance:** figure computation tests;
engine walk per figure." This pull request adds UX-29 to its *Depends on* (decided by the
coordinator, 2026-10-21). § UX-29 (#1670): "An entry's level is hidden when its type is not offered
**or an ancestor's level is hidden**; a type with several system parents … is offered when **any**
offered level may hold it."

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 **The server computes every figure; the client draws it.** — decided by: worker, from
  `offline-sync.md` §4.2 and §1, P-12 and P-08. Rejected: computing from the replica (forbidden
  online, no entries in the navigation, nothing for MCP or `hubctl`, two answers).
- [x] D2 **The figure is a field of the container read**, `Container.figure` (`CollectionFigure`),
  answered by `GetContainer` and `ListContainers`. — decided by: worker. Rejected: an endpoint of its
  own (one request per collection in the navigation, a new use case on every door). The `ETag` stays
  the container's version; nothing answers `304`.
- [x] D3 **What each figure counts.** — decided by: worker, within UC-WRK-29, `design-system.md` §7
  and the coordinator's review answers (3, 4). Every figure counts the collection's entries, never a
  person's; only entries that are not hidden (D17); trashed entries never.
  * **OPEN** — `open`: open, unarchived entries directly under the collection (its rows).
  * **FIELD_SUM** — `open` as OPEN; `sum`: the sum of the named field over those entries, counted
    only where the entry's level carries `CUSTOM_FIELDS` and the value's definition is a live
    `NUMBER` (D7); `summed`: how many carry a value (the client says "about" when fewer than `open`).
    No unit: none is stored.
  * **DUE** — `due`: open, unarchived entries at any visible level whose level carries `DUE_DATE`
    and that are **due by the end of the reader's day**: a timed due date whose instant is before
    the end of the reader's local day; an all-day due date whose **calendar date** — the local date
    of `due_at` in `due_time_zone`, UTC where none is stored — is on or before the reader's local
    date. Two meanings of "due today" exist and are kept apart: *the reader's today* (this figure,
    and *Today*'s list) and *the entry's own day in its zone* (`due.ts`'s overdue rule, which this
    figure does not use). One number: what is due, never a count of what is late.
  * **RHYTHM** — `days`: the dates of the reader's current week (`week_start` and zone) on which at
    least one visible entry of the collection was completed, archived ones included; `week_start`
    is answered beside it. **It counts the collection, not a person**: whoever completed, the day
    counts once, and no figure says who.
  * **PROGRESS** — `done`, `total`: completed and all visible leaves (no visible, untrashed child),
    **archived leaves counted as they stand**, as the roll-up (I-W5) counts children — archiving
    finished leaves does not lower the share. The client shows the share rounded down (100 % only
    when everything is done) and draws nothing while `total` is 0.
  * **NONE** — no `figure`. **FLOW** — no `figure` until UX-26.
  Every figure answers `today` (the reader's date) so a client knows when it went stale.

  document.
- [x] D5 **A list is a collection whose levels are one, and it never shows a percentage.** — decided
  by: worker, reading check 2 with UC-WRK-25 check 1; refined by the coordinator (review 5, 6).

    the levels in force under the write's `LayerShared` reading — for a collection with its hub

    narrowed on this or another layer, the installation's bound narrowed, a workspace lock — is

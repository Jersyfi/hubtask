- [x] D3 **The person's objection is the consent record**, purpose `ai_processing` (`privacy.PurposeAi`): in force when the newest record for the account is a withdrawal. Switching off is a new granted record, source `user`, through a new self-only `GiveConsent` (`POST /privacy/consents:give`) — an administrator cannot (P-14), so a withdrawal an administrator recorded by mistake is undone by the person. `account.ai_consent` stays unused (dead column, a finding). Worker; rejected: deleting or rewriting the withdrawal (the record of what was allowed when is the point, UC-PRV-05 *Goal*); a flag on the account (a second truth beside the record).
- [x] D4 **One exclusion, applied where the material is read.** `integration.KeptOut` (application layer) is read in the same read-only transaction as the material: `{accounts: map[id]Reason, names}`. Reason today is `OBJECTED` — position `OFFERED`, `CountPeople > 1`, the newest `ai_processing` record a withdrawal. Applied: an entry whose `created_by` is kept out is not material (collection summaries skip it; a single-entry question about it is refused, D6); a comment whose author is kept out is not material; then `Scrub` replaces, in all remaining text, each kept-out person's display name and each of its parts of three letters or more, their sign-in name and their address, matched whole-word and case-insensitively, with the fixed token `[person]`. Called in exactly three places — the producer's `ask`, `AiTranslate`, `EmbedItems` — and the search's query embedding is gated by the caller (D7). "What they authored" is what the model records an author for: the entries they created (title, notes), their comments, their template request text; structure they named (labels, columns, containers) is the workspace's, not theirs. Worker.
- [x] D5 **PH-09 adds a reason, not a second mechanism.** `KeptOut` is built from a list of `KeptOutSource`s; PH-03 ships `ObjectionSource`; PH-09 adds `RestrictionSource` (`Accounts.Restricted`, reason `RESTRICTED`), which — unlike an objection — holds under `NOT_OFFERED` and in a one-person workspace (Art. 18).

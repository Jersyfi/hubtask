## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 **The terms get a version, set beside their link at the level that sets the link.** A
  `terms_version` (optional, at most 64 characters, no control characters) at the installation and the
  workspace; it resolves **with** `terms_url`, from the same level and under the same lock — a
  workspace cannot put its version on the installation's link (`auth.terms_version_without_link`),
  and a locked installation link locks its version. Decided by worker; rejected: a version resolved on
  its own (a workspace could re-label somebody else's terms), and a server-stamped date as the only
  version (UC-ID-19 names "version 3", which an administrator chooses).
- [x] D2 **What counts as "agreed": the link and the version in force.** The account stores
  `terms_url`, `terms_version` and `terms_agreed_at`; it is current when link and version equal the
  ones in force. Changing either asks everybody once at their next sign-in, and the settings screen
  says so beside both fields. Terms are "set" when a terms link is in force, with or without a version
  — so where an installation or workspace has a terms link today, every person meets the step once
  after this lands, which is what check 2 asks of somebody with no agreement on record. Decided by
  worker; rejected: asking only once a version is set (check 1 says "where terms are set", and a link
  is the terms), and a fingerprint instead of the link (the stored link tells an auditor what was
  agreed to).
- [x] D3 **One helper decides between the session and the `TERMS` step** — every site that opens a
  session after a sign-in calls it instead of `openSessionVia`; it reads the terms in force through the
  existing resolver and mints a `TERMS` pending credential carrying the session's method, provider and
  device hint, so the session it opens at agreement is the one the sign-in would have opened. The step
  is **last**, after every proof and after `PASSWORD_CHANGE`. Persons only (`USER`). Decided by worker
  — identity.md §4.2's chain gains one link.
- [x] D4 **Declining is the card's**: no route; the card drops the pending credential (it can do
  nothing else and dies within five minutes) and says whom to ask — "the administrators of
  {workspace}" for a workspace's terms, "whoever runs this installation" for the installation's.
  Nothing is recorded: declining changes nothing. Decided by worker; rejected: a decline route (a
  write that records a non-event, and one more public route).
- [x] D5 **The password invitation takes the agreement in its body** (`terms_url`, `terms_version`, as
  shown) and refuses without it (`422 auth.terms_not_agreed`) or against other terms (`409
  auth.terms_changed`) before the token is looked up, so nothing is spent. Decided by worker;
  rejected: activating and then asking at a step (the person would hold an active account they never
  agreed for).
- [x] D6 **The provider invitation carries the agreement on the flow** (`oidc_flow` columns), stored
  at activation; without it the arrival meets the `TERMS` step. Decided by worker.
- [x] D7 **A managed account agrees at its first sign-in**, as the `TERMS` step after its
  `PASSWORD_CHANGE` — it has no invitation card (PH-06); the person, not the administrator who created
  it, agrees. Decided by worker.
- [x] D8 **The setup's owner is not asked at the setup.** On a fresh installation no terms exist
  unless the operator's own instance file set them; the setup stays one step, and the owner meets the
  step at their next sign-in where terms are in force. Decided by worker; rejected: a box on the setup
  card (the person setting up is the one who sets the terms).
- [x] D9 **No step for a non-sign-in**: service accounts, tokens, the OAuth token endpoint, a refresh
  and an elevation open no new sign-in; UC-ID-19 asks "at their next sign-in". Decided by worker.
- [x] D10 **`hubctl login` answers `TERMS`** (P-08): shows link and version, asks; `--agree-terms`
  agrees without asking. Decided by worker.
- [x] D11 **The agreement box is required, not a disabled button**: pressing *Set password* or a
  provider button without the tick shows the field error at the box (P-05 — no control disabled for a
  reason the screen can say instead). Decided by worker.
- [x] D12 **Migration 0140, expand only**: `account.terms_url`, `terms_version`, `terms_agreed_at`;
  `auth_pending` purpose `TERMS` and `session_method`, `session_via`; `oidc_flow.terms_url`,
  `terms_version`. Settings need no DDL (jsonb; the instance key `legal.terms_version` fits 0099's key
  check). An old process during the rollout ignores the columns and opens sessions without the step;
  the next sign-in asks. Decided by worker.
- [x] D13 **Message codes**: `en` always; `de` for `auth.*`, `app.redeem.*`, `app.sign_in.*`,
  `app.signin_settings.*`, `app.instance.*`, all translated there. Decided by the template's rule.
- [x] D14 **Finding #1289 is fixed here as steps 9–10** (the brief allows it, the same card):
  `POST /auth/invitations:inspect`, public, rate-limited as the redemption, answers the workspace's
  display name and the invited address for a token that would redeem, and the redemption's one
  refusal otherwise; it spends nothing. Test first, own commits. Decided by worker; the coordinator may
  cut both steps out without touching the rest.

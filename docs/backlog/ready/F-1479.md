## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 `Held` counts, in all three phases, every matched entry the pass could not act on because
  something will still keep it on the next pass: a legal hold, and in phase two a retained
  descendant. `Outcome.Held`'s comment says so for the rule sweep too — decided by: worker; the
  issue's proposal and #1452's reading. Rejected: leaving the descendant out, as the trash pass
  does — in phase two the parent keeps its marking and is read again, so it spins the job like a
  held one.
- [x] D2 `Matched` keeps its meaning (every matched row): the run log, the audit summary and the
  metric are unchanged — decided by: worker; as #1468.
- [x] D3 The starvation of due rows behind a batch of held or non-matching rows (§ 1) is not fixed
  here: it needs an offset on three candidate statements and on `MarkedDue`, through the `Marking`
  port, and a paged read in all three phases. That is #1451's fix for another pass, bigger than a
  step, and not what #1479 reports. It is finding #1488, filed with this record —
  decided by: worker. Rejected: fixing it in this pull request (two concerns, one of them a
  query change the issue does not name).

# F-1371 — The role ceiling holds at every door: groups and revocations

**Task:** F-1371 · issues #1371 and #1372 (findings) · milestone "CL — A clean slate" (no Delivers line)
**Checked against:** `origin/main` at `220e311d` on 2026-10-14
**Verdict:** ready

A finding fix, not a milestone task. The commits carry `Task: F-1371`, so `make gate-pr` reads this
record. It exists because the fix widens an authorisation rule (who may take a role away, who may
change a group) and adds a response field to `api/openapi.yaml`. Both issues were found while
building #1288 (`F-1288.md`, D5) and are one rule: the ceiling `service.MayGrant` holds wherever a
role is placed **or taken away**, directly or through a group.

**(d)** Rule 2: every refusal is `access.Service.Authorize` with `Grants`; the use cases only say
which roles are at stake; the web app draws what the server answered. Rule 10: codes and
identifiers only in the entries. Rule 11: contract first, `make generate`, `make api-client`. P-05:
`members_changeable` is computed by `GrantableRoles`, the function behind the refusal, over every
role the group holds; a revoke button is drawn only for a role in `grantable_roles`, which is the
condition the revoke now refuses on. Known traps: *a write on a refusal path is rolled back* — the
group's roles are read and the ceiling asked **before** the write transaction, so the DENIED entry
stands (as `Authorize` is meant to be called); *`Handler.Invoke` skips validation* — refusal tests
call `Execute`; *a capability test can ask itself* — the `members_changeable` test asserts concrete
answers per actor with a fake authoriser, the integration test asserts the real resolution; *a
premise can be wrong* — § 1 (one false premise found, D7); *a read-then-write without a lock* — two
writers: an owner grants `OWNER` to a group while an administrator adds themselves to it, the
ceiling having been asked when the group held less. Guarded by a lock on the group row
(`Groups.Lock`, `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`): the member change and the deletion take it in their write
transaction and read the group's roles again under it, and a grant to a group takes it before it
writes. If the roles under the lock are not all among those the ceiling was asked for, the write is
refused with `groups.roles_changed` (409, a conflict to retry); the loser of the race either waits
and sees the new role, or is seen by the other. The member comparison of D3 is made under the same
lock.

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 The rule: taking a role away needs what handing it out needs — `MANAGE_MEMBERS` at the scope
  and a role along the path at least as strong (`service.MayGrant`); changing who is in a group, or
  deleting it, places or takes away every role the group holds, so each of them is held to the same
  ceiling at its own scope — decided by: worker, after #1371's and #1372's proposals and
  `domain-model.md` §3.2 ("this holds for every door that places a role"). Written in
  `domain-model.md` §3.2.
- [x] D2 Where: `access.Request.Grants` carries the role at stake for a revocation too (its comment
  says "places or takes away"); the group use cases ask `Authorize` once per distinct
  (scope, role) the group holds — decided by: worker. Rejected: a second request field
  (`Revokes`) — the check is identical, and two names for one rule would drift.
- [x] D3 An unchanged member list is not judged against the group's roles — decided by: worker; a
  client sending the whole object would otherwise be refused for a change it did not make (the
  shape of #1381).
- [x] D4 How the client learns it: an additive `members_changeable` on `GroupDetail`, the answer to
  "may this reader change who is in the group, and delete it" — decided by: worker (additive, within
  AGENTS.md). Revoke needs no new field: a row's role against the scope's `grantable_roles` already
  answers it. Rejected: listing the group's grants on the group (a wider disclosure to every member,
  who may read groups).
- [x] D5 The revoke's ceiling comes before the step-up, as the grant's does — a proof spent on a
  refused act would be burnt — decided by: `F-1288.md` D3.
- [x] D6 No ADR: a consequence of the existing role order — decided by: worker.
- [x] D7 Out of scope, reported as findings rather than fixed here — decided by: worker: (a) there is
  no last-owner guard (§ 1); (b) changing the members of a group holding `OWNER`, or deleting it,
  asks no step-up although identity.md §16.3 demands one for granting or revoking `OWNER` — after
  this fix only an owner can do it, and adding `step_up_token` to the group routes is a contract and
  screen change of its own; (c) `IssueStartPassword` lets an administrator give a managed account
  holding `OWNER` a new start password (`IssueStartPassword.go`), the same escalation by another
  door. F-1288 D5(a) called the revocation ceiling "a product question"; it is decided here as
  worker because #1372, filed since, states it as the proposal and domain-model.md §3.2 already
  frames the ceiling as holding at "every door that places a role" — taking away is its mirror.
- [x] D8 A new conflict code `groups.roles_changed` (409, `en` and `de`) for the race of § 2(d) —
  decided by: worker.

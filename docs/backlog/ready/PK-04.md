## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 One helper decides whether a second factor is held - the app armed, or a current key **and
  the request at the relying party's very host** - read by `pendingPurposeFor`, the three LINK doors
  and the invitation's proof. Where a key cannot sign (a reset or an invitation redeemed at the
  installation's host in multi mode, #1337), a key-only account meets what it meets today: no factor
  step, or `ENROLL` under the rule. Nothing a person observes changes there, so it waits on nobody;
  it ends with #1337 — decided by: identity.md §23.2, §23.3, milestone Decision 9.
- [x] D2 Every challenge is an `auth_pending` row of its own purpose (§23.3): `PASSKEY_FACTOR` (the
  account, minted beside the `TOTP` credential and carried in the 202), `PASSKEY_ENROLL` (the account
  and the user handle, minted by the setup's options call with the `ENROLL` credential),
  `PASSKEY_STEP_UP` (the account and the session). Migration 0170 widens the purpose check and the
  two shape checks; expand-only. A challenge is spent only by its own door, for its account (and
  session) — decided by: identity.md §23.3.
- [x] D3 Presence suffices at the factor step and the step-up; UV is read only where a passkey opens
  the account alone — decided by: #1521, §23.1.
- [x] D4 `signed_in_with = PASSWORD_PASSKEY` where a key was the second factor after the password;
  a link the mailbox proved stays `OIDC` with its provider; the change step and `TERMS` carry it on;
  the step-up's method `PASSKEY`, audited as such — decided by: worker, UC-ID-06 check 2.
- [x] D5 A refused key at the factor step is the code's refusal (`auth.mfa_code_invalid`), counted on
  the account's `mfa:` subject, the pending credential and its challenge kept for another try; at the
  step-up the step-up's refusal on its subject — decided by: identity.md §4.3.
- [x] D6 The setup during sign-in registers with the `ENROLL` credential, no step-up (an `ENROLL`
  credential exists only where no factor is held - `resolveMfaCaller`'s reasoning); refused under the
  account lock where a factor appeared meanwhile; the same verifier, limit, codes (only where none),
  audit and way-in notice as PK-01 and PK-02; it ends as `ConfirmTotp` does. Offered only where the
  relying party exists and the request is at its host: the `ENROLL` 202 says so — decided by: UC-ID-22
  check 8, Decision 9.
- [x] D7 The step-up's order `PASSWORD`, `TOTP`, `PASSKEY`, `RECOVERY`, `PROVIDER`; `RECOVERY` wherever
  a factor is held and codes are left. `Methods` cannot see the host: the options call refuses
  another host (409 `auth.passkey_unavailable`) and the prompt then offers the other ways — decided
  by: §16.1, §23.4, P-05.
- [x] D8 The switch leaves factors alone — decided by: milestone Decision 6.
- [x] D9 The factor step's card draws what `methods` names: a code field only with `TOTP`, *Use my
  security key* with `PASSKEY`, the recovery code with `RECOVERY`, and words where nothing is left —
  decided by: worker, P-05.

Nothing waits on the owner.

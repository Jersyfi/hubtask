findings, and what changed:

4. The version belongs in `If-Match` and the existing `admin.tenant_version_moved` → adopted; the
   quota code's `conflict` is a finding fixed in step 3.
5. Workspace administrators raise the version → in § 1, decided as D9.
6. Pending deletion was refused against the milestone's matrix → allowed until the purge (D10).
7. D5 left `enforce` with plans no door could remove → the AI models' precedent.
8. PG-7 would fail → catalogue rows in step 5.
9. The three lists are the two maps and §2.1, and they already disagree → step 2; schema.sql and
   grants named.
10. `Pending.go` and the locales would break step 4 → named in the step.
11. Step 6 was far too big → split into steps 7–9.

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 Semantics: once; only while open and before the original deadline; at most three months after receipt; reason `COMPLEXITY` or `NUMBER_OF_REQUESTS`; informed date required; Hubtask never writes to the person — decided by data-protection.md §4.1, UC-PRV-01 *Where it ends*.
- [x] D2 Storage: `due_at` stays the deadline in force and takes the extended date; new nullable `original_due_at` (also the "once" marker), `extension_reason`, `informed_on` (`date`), a CHECK that all three are set or none, and one on the reason's values — decided by worker: every reader (list order, cursor, `DueBefore`, the overdue count, `dsr_open_idx`, `standingOf`, A-19) follows unchanged, and an old pod in a rolling update reads the right deadline and writes none of the new columns (`Privacy.sql:33-42`). Rejected: a separate `extended_due_at` read through `COALESCE` in four queries and a new index.
- [x] D3 "Three months after receipt" is a calendar period in the workspace's own zone (`Workspaces.Find`; an unknown zone falls back to UTC as `InstantiateTemplate.go:545-549` does): the day of `received_at` plus three months, clamped to the last day of that month, is the last day an extension may name (Reg. 1182/71 Art. 3(2)(c)); the new deadline must also be later than the current one. Counted from `received_at` as stored (§ 1, #1177). Written into data-protection.md §4.1 in step 13 — decided by worker. Rejected: `AddDate(0,3,0)` (30 Nov → 2 Mar, past 28 Feb); the same UTC clock time (lenient by hours where a clamp collapses, e.g. received 31 Jan 00:30 CET, and by days behind UTC around 28 Feb/1 Mar); 90 days. A zone changed since receipt: today's zone counts.
- [x] D4 Both inputs are calendar days, `due_on` and `informed_on`: plain strings in the descriptor (no registry format), `format: date` in the contract, parsed in `invoke` like `anchor_date`. The server makes `due_on` the deadline 23:59:59 of that day in the workspace's zone — the client needs no zone and sends the day it shows. `informed_on` may not lie before the day of receipt or after today, both in the workspace's zone — decided by worker: §4.1 names a date; a time of day is invented precision, and one conversion in one place cannot disagree with itself.
- [x] D5 Its own use case `ExtendDataSubjectRequest`, `POST /privacy/requests/{requestId}:extend` with `Idempotency-Key`, `Destructive: false` — decided by worker: api-guidelines.md §2 "Actions use the suffix pattern" (`:62`); folded into the update it would inherit `Destructive: true` (`Requests.go:668`) and an agent would need `agent:destructive` (`core/application/usecase/Registry.go:245-283`) to move a date. (The first draft's reason — one descriptor, one action — was wrong: the update writes three, `Requests.go:272-282`.)
- [x] D6 Refusals per door. The descriptor declares `due_on`, `reason`, `informed_on` required and `reason` an enum. **REST:** the handler passes a field only when it is not the generated zero value, so a missing field reaches the registry as absent → 400 `usecase.input_invalid` with `usecase.field_required` at its path (`Input.go:296-303,314-315`); an unknown reason → `usecase.field_not_in_enum` (`:335-341`); a malformed date fails decoding → 400 `request.body_malformed` (`ContainerController.go:135`). **MCP, automation:** the same registry codes; a malformed date → `privacy.due_on_malformed` / `privacy.informed_on_malformed`, the pattern of `parseInstant` (`Output.go:70-79`). **Domain** (every door): `privacy.already_extended`, `privacy.extension_after_deadline`, `privacy.extension_too_long` (param `latest`, `YYYY-MM-DD`), `privacy.extension_not_later`, `privacy.informed_on_out_of_range`, `privacy.extension_reason_invalid` (a guard for callers below the registry); a closed case reuses `privacy.request_closed`.

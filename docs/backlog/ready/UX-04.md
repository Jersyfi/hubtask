**(d) Standing rules.**
* Rules 8, 13; voice-and-tone §1–§3: every sentence a code in `en` and `de`; a notice says why and
  what to do, and to someone who cannot do it, whom to ask (§3.1); the bar's verbs follow §2.4, §2.5.
* Rule 15: the toast's dwell is a new duration token, `primitive.duration.dwell` (D8); no value in the app.
* P-05 for every value that says "can be done now": the role (`holds`, `mayDo`, `mayModerate`,
  `grantableAt`), the archive (the collection's `effective_archived`, the entry's `archived_at` —
  **not** an archived parent, finding 4), the capability (`carries`). Broken-when: no new control
  leads to a refusal the screen could know.
* P-11: a failed save ends busy and keeps what was typed.
* P-13, rule 3: no state told by colour alone; with finding 4 no row is greyed without a notice.
* Known traps: *a live region created already filled is not announced* — the host renders `Toast`

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 The palette, 1.18:1 and 1.36:1, soft elevation for cards, colour for hubs and collections
  and never for a level — decided by: owner, 2026-10-20, #1624 → design-system.md §1, §6, §7,
  milestone UX Decision 4, ADR-0087 §5.
- [x] D2 **`bg.raised`** (role `surface`): light = `neutral.0` (white is the ceiling; the shadow
  carries the depth), dark = `neutral.875` `#242B3E`. It is the background of what stands above a
  `bg.surface` plane: `Menu`, `Popover`, `Dialog`, `Drawer`, `CoachMark`, `SideNav`'s flyout, the web app's `SearchMenu` and `SearchLine`
  dropdowns, `RuleEditorView`'s `.sheetbar`, and `WorkItemCard` on its column. **Not**: `Tooltip`
  (an inverse surface, rule 1's overlay of its own), `AppBar` and `BottomBar` (the frame, a plane of
  `bg.surface` without elevation, §6 rule 1), `DetailPane` (a pane of the frame beside the list, not
  above it). `Toast` always draws on its tone's opaque surface; `PageHeader`'s folded primary is a filled
  button that already floats with `elevation.overlay` and gains only the glow (D7). The rule
  canvas's cards stand on the canvas and are D8's case. A card on the canvas is
  `bg.surface` with `elevation.raised`. Hover and pressed are re-stepped so each stays visible on
  both bases and on the canvas, pressed a step past hover; `surface-sunken` and
  `status.neutral.surface` are re-stepped in both modes (§ 2(b)) — decided by: worker and
  coordinator (finding 4, 5); rejected: a raised step in light (nothing is lighter than white), and
  every card `bg.raised` (two shades of card on one page with nothing to tell them apart).
- [x] D3 **The contrast test composites every translucent surface over each opaque base** —
  `bg.canvas`, `bg.surface`, `bg.raised`, `bg.surface-sunken` — in both modes, and measures every
  foreground on each result; the ambient tints stay a canvas variant. The dark status, AI and
  `accent.*-subtle` surfaces become opaque soft steps (the owner's values), so `bg.glass` is the one
  translucent surface left; `Toast` draws on an opaque tone surface or on `bg.raised` — decided by:
  coordinator (finding 2).
- [x] D4 **Families and roles.** Primitive families by hue, each a ramp 50–950 derived in OKLCH
  through the owner's values (hex in the file, the notation the test parses): `indigo`,
  `raspberry`, `danger`, `mint`, `amber`, `sky`, `violet`, and `neutral` (re-tinted cool; steps
  0–1000 kept for `QrCode`). `blue`, `ember`, `iris`, `success`, `warning` are removed.
  - **Danger keeps its own vivid red** (`danger`, the old ramp adjusted to OKLCH hue 32°):
    `status.danger.*` and `text.danger` use it. **Raspberry is the signature only** —
    `accent.signature*`, `ambient.signature`, `rim.signature` — and keeps "carries no system
    meaning" in its description. The danger suite holds their hue distance at 17.5° (measured
    17.56° / 18.97°) — decided by: coordinator (finding 1); the brand must not read as an alarm.
  - Per mode: `accent.primary` indigo 600 / 400, `-hover` 700 / 300, `-pressed` 800 / 200,
    `-subtle` 100 / 950; `text.brand` indigo 700 / 300; `focus.ring` indigo 500 / 300;
    `accent.signature` raspberry 600 / 400, `-subtle` 100 / 950; `status.info` sky, `.success` mint,
    `.warning` amber, `.danger` danger, `.neutral` neutral; `ai.*` violet. Each role's step is the
    test's verdict: where a starting value fails (light amber, mint, sky under `text.inverse`; the
    concept's strong border on the canvas; dark raised at 1.3586) the role takes the next step of the
    same family — decided by: worker (finding 8).
  - `rim.blue`, `rim.ember` become `rim.primary`, `rim.signature`, named as `ambient.*` are.
- [x] D5 **What a status is, and how the scans classify it — by token, not by selector.** A status
  is the standing state of a thing shown beside it (health, connection, over a limit, open,
  trashed, broken, needs attention, done, checked, invalid). Written into design-system.md §6
  rule 3. The scans (`build/lint-status.js`, run by `test/status.test.js` in the design system
  over `src/`, and by both clients' `pnpm lint` over their own `src/`, as the literal and direction
  gates are) fail:
  1. a rule using any `status.<tone>.*` token unless the same rule, or a rule on the same element
     reached by a prefix of its selector, sets a background from `status.<tone>.surface` or
     `status.<tone>.accent` — except inside the status components themselves (`Badge`, `Banner`,
     `Toast`, `Callout`, `RunStatusBadge`);
  2. `text.danger`, `text.success`, `text.warning` on any property but `color`, or on a selector
     carrying a state attribute (any attribute selector but the closed non-state list:
     `data-destructive` and `IconButton`/`Button`'s `data-tone` — a kind of action — and
     `ParameterTable`'s `data-required` — a property of a parameter);
  3. both rules through one level of custom-property indirection (`--x: var(--status-…)`, then
     `color: var(--x)`) and through Svelte `style:` directives.
  A refusal sentence (`.failure`, `.refusal`, a field's message) stays `text.danger` on `color`: it
  is text, and rule 3 is about colour standing alone. Each false-negative shape has its own planted
  case in the self-test — decided by: coordinator (finding 3); rejected: a selector allow-list (it
  goes stale) and converting all 116 (a pink box under every form).
- [x] D6 **Labels read as colour**, measured against the surfaces they sit on: OKLab distance of
  each coloured label's `bg` from `bg.surface` and `bg.raised` ≥ 0.09 light, 0.08 dark (weakest
  measured 0.0944 / 0.0804; the old labels 0.0621 / 0.0190); a dark label's `bg` is never darker
  than `bg.raised` (no hole). `slate` is exempt from the distance with its reason in the test — the
  one label meant to read as no colour; its pair stays measured. Ten keys, same names, same order —
  decided by: coordinator (finding 7, 12), the reviewer's alternative.
- [x] D7 **The glow** (`elevation.glow` per mode) is worn only by `PageHeader`'s one primary action,
  and not while that action is `aria-disabled`. It is decorative: a shadow token, not a colour role;
  the fill and the focus ring are measured on the surface beneath — decided by: worker (finding 8).
- [x] D8 **Cards on the canvas are raised**: a standalone card or panel — `bg.surface`, a hairline
  and a radius on `bg.canvas` — wears `elevation.raised`; a row inside a card, a form section and
  the frame do not (§6 rule 1). Design-system cards (`WorkItemCard`, `AutomationRuleCard`,
  `ApiEndpointCard`, `JumbleInboxItem`) and every such web-app panel, the rule canvas's cards
  included — decided by: worker.
- [x] D9 **The marks are UX-06's.** The website mark and the workbench wordmark story get a
  one-line interim repoint of their fills to `--accent-primary`, `--accent-primary-hover` and
  `--accent-signature`, which UX-06 replaces; `Foundations.svelte`'s prose and its reading pairs
  (with `bg.raised`) are this task's — decided by: coordinator (finding 9).
- [x] D10 **The website** follows the tokens: both dark hand-back blocks in `site.css` rename
  `--rim-blue` → `--rim-primary`, `--rim-ember` → `--rim-signature`, and gain every new property its
  components read (`--bg-raised`); `site.css:724,832` read `--rim-signature`. `check-theme.test.js`
  is the proof — decided by: coordinator (finding 10).

No question on the owner's list is open: no rule deviates, no dependency, no API field, no change
to a use case's *Goal*, checks or *Where it ends*.

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 **The access.** A new entity `work.EmergencyAccess` (`private_hub_access`: id, hub, opener,
  reason, `opened_at`, `ends_at = opened_at + 1h`). `OpenPrivateHub` (`POST
  /private-hubs/{hubId}/accesses`, body `{reason}`, `StepUp` on the descriptor) asks, in this order:
  a person; `OWNER` on the workspace (`access.Authorize`, the refusal recorded); then in one
  transaction the hub's row (`HubLocks`), private, not held by the opener, no running access of
  theirs; then writes the access, the members' records and jobs (D7), the audit entry. Decided by
  ADR-0073 §4; the shape by worker. Rejected: a membership granted for an hour (it would be a grant
  in the members list, audited and revoked like one, and a revocation job could fail and leave it).
- [x] D2 **Not renewable** means no extension and no second access while one runs (409 naming the
  end); after it ends a new one is a new act — new proof, new reason, everybody told again. No
  early end is built: the hour is the bound and ending it early protects nobody. Decided by worker,
  after identity.md §17.2's "never extended (a second opening is new)".
- [x] D3 **What it opens.** While it runs, the authoriser treats the private hub as shared **for the
  opener only**: their workspace membership counts there, and only while it is `OWNER` (a demotion
  ends the access's effect at once). Read in `privacyOf`, one statement asked only when a private
  hub is on the path (`RunningAccess(account, hubs, now)`), so every reader follows and no other
  question costs anything. Writes are allowed — taking an orphaned hub over needs a grant and a
  restore (ADR-0073 §5) — and are in the trail as every act. Decided by PH-04 D17 and ADR-0073 §5.
- [x] D4 **Only for the person, now.** The hour must leave nothing behind. The access counts only for
  an actor marked *present* — a request through REST or MCP (`hubctl` is REST) answered while the
  person waits — and the presentation adapters set that mark (a fact about the request, decided on
  nowhere but in `access`). It never counts for the device replica (the sync services clear the
  mark: no `ACCESS_REVOKED` is then owed when the hour ends), the calendar feed (a synthetic actor),
  a job run later (built without the mark), nor in `Hidden` (rules, subscriptions, notifications).
  Fail closed: an actor somebody forgets to mark sees nothing. Decided by worker. Rejected: counting
  everywhere and writing `ACCESS_REVOKED` at the end (a scheduled pass, a minute of a replica
  holding a diary after the hour, a feed subscription keeping it in a calendar app).
- [x] D5 **No domain event.** Nothing subscribes to it: a webhook or rule seeing it would carry news
  of a private hub to somebody outside it. The audit entry and the members' records are its
  traces. Decided by worker.
- [x] D6 **The trail and the reason.** `container.emergency_access_opened` (target the hub, `ends_at`
  `Open`, the reason `SENSITIVE` — "changed" and a fingerprint). The reason is written to the
  members, often about somebody's health, and the trail is read by administrators and auditors who
  are not; the members read it on the access (D8) and in the mail. Unlike §17.2, whose reason is
  addressed to the administrators who read the trail. Decided by worker (rule 10, audit.md §4).
- [x] D7 **The notification, and the rolling window.** A new category `EMERGENCY_ACCESS`; the record
  gains a fourth subject column, `private_hub_access_id` (the one-subject check widened), so the
  mail reads opener, time, end and reason from the access at delivery and the record holds no
  content. Recorded inside the opening's transaction (`RecordEmergencyAccess`, after
  `RecordPrivateHubTrashed`), for every member as PH-04 D9 defines one (a person neither anonymised
  nor deleted holding a role on the hub or below it). Delivered by **its own job kind**,
  `notification.emergency_access`, handled by `DeliverNotification` like the others: an old pod
  has no handler for it and puts it back (`queue.handler_missing`), so it can never render the
  category with the assignment's words; the kind's `max_attempts` is set so that a rollout's claims
  cannot use them up (checked against the backoff in step 6). Decided by worker, from PH-03's
  finding. The mail names the hub only where *Include the title* is on (data-protection.md §9); the
  time is in the recipient's zone. The access does not wait for the mail: delivery retries, and the
  record and the hub's page (D8) already say it.
- [x] D8 **A member without a mailbox is told on the hub.** `ListPrivateHubAccesses` (`GET
  /private-hubs/{hubId}/accesses`, `READ` on the hub) answers each access; the hub's page shows its
  members a notice for every access of the last 30 days — who, when, until, why — and the opener,
  during the hour, a notice that the members were told and when it closes. A child's managed account
  (ADR-0074 §5) and anybody whose mail failed is told the next time they open the hub. Decided by
  worker; "notified at once" is met for every member with an address, and for one without as soon
  as any channel the product has can reach them (UC-NOT-06 is not built). **Named to the
  coordinator** (question 3).
- [x] D9 **Always on.** `EMERGENCY_ACCESS` is not suppressible, as the invitation is not; the
  profile shows it as always on and says why. UC-ID-16 *Where it ends*: "there is no silent variant
  for anyone" — a member's own preference would be one. UC-NOT-03's *Goal* ("the one message that
  lets them in never switchable off") stays true; its *Story* lists the new kind. *Include the
  title* stays switchable. Decided by worker. **Named to the coordinator** (question 2).
- [x] D10 **The setting is a sign-in switch** — PH-06's way (D7 there): `private_hubs` joins the
  identity rule's switches, default on, off the stricter, `Effective(instance, plan, workspace)`,
  locked with its origin, stored in the workspace's `sign_in_policy` and `instance_setting`
  `sign_in.private_hubs`, written with the step-up every switch takes; `hubctl admin instance` and
  the instance file carry it through their generic map. Read by `AuthorizeOwnPrivateHub`,
  `SetHubPrivacy` (marking only) and `private_hubs_offered`. Off refuses new private hubs
  (`containers.private_hubs_off`, 403) and changes nothing else. Drawn on the *Sign-in* settings
  screen as its own section *Private hubs* with value, source and lock, and on `/instance` beside
  `managed_accounts`. Decided by the coordinator's instruction and ADR-0073 §6; the placement by
  worker. Rejected: a new instance area and a tenant column (a second place for the same three
  levels, P-06). **Named to the coordinator** (question 1).
- [x] D11 **Personal data.** `private_hub_access`: the opener (an identifier) and the reason
  (`PERSONAL_CONTENT`, possibly health) — purpose: the members' record of who opened their hub;
  kept as long as the hub, deleted with it (cascade); the opener's erasure leaves the row with the
  opener anonymised as every attributed act (data-protection.md §5), the reason kept — it is the
  members' record. A data-catalogue row. Decided by worker.
- [x] D12 **The archive carries the accesses**: export and import with the hub, every restore mode
  (an access whose hub is not restored is skipped), a round trip. A restore that dropped them would
  erase the members' record. Decided by worker (tenant-export.md §11, additive, format 1).
- [x] D13 **Migration 0138, expand only.** The table under `tenant_isolation`; the two category
  checks and the one-subject check replaced by wider ones added `NOT VALID` and validated in a
  second statement (no long lock on `notification`); the new column nullable, its foreign key
  `NOT VALID` then validated. An old pod reads, writes and claims nothing new (D7); its window rows
  are § 2(b)'s. Decided by worker.
- [x] D14 **Codes.** New: `containers.emergency_reason_required`, `_reason_too_long`,
  `_not_private`, `_not_needed`, `_running`, `containers.private_hubs_off`,
  `email.emergency_access.*` (and `.withheld`), the switch's `app.signin_settings.*` and
  `app.instance.*` texts, `app.private_hubs.*` (action, dialog, notices), `app.profile.category_EMERGENCY_ACCESS`.
  `en` always; `de` for each, every family being translated (`locales/de.json`). Decided by worker.
- [x] D15 **Outside this task.** A screen for the trail's entry beyond the existing audit view;
  telling the workspace's other owners (ADR-0073 names the members); an early end (D2). Decided by
  worker.

Nothing is open for the owner: ADR-0073 decided the shape; D1–D15 change no *Goal*, *How to
check* or *Where it ends*. Three choices are named to the coordinator for review (§ 5).

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 **Split check 10.** PH-01 carries "not erased or anonymised but restricted; everything no
  hold covers is erased as usual" — the account is `RESTRICTED`, the one Art. 18 state
  (data-protection.md §4). What that state keeps the data out of is a new task, **PH-09**, carrying
  UC-PRV-03 (10) as well: the automation engine and the suggestion service read the restriction
  (UC-PRV-04 checks 4 and 5, findings that block it), the prompt builder treats a restricted
  person's content as an objecting person's (PH-03), and the exports follow the owner's answer to
  O1. Written into `milestone-PH.md` in this commit — decided by worker (`docs/backlog/README.md`,
  "Tasks move"): the restriction half needs the owner and a mechanism PH-03 builds; the hold half
  needs neither, so PH-01 is `ready` and nothing is stacked on a waiting task. Rejected: carrying
  it here (the task waits on the owner, and builds PH-03's exclusion twice).
- [x] D2 **What each step of the erasure keeps.** A located row is kept when a hold covers its
  entry (`Holds.Blocking` with the entry's hub, collection, path and contributors): the person's
  comments in `FULL_DELETE`, their assignments (a field of the held entry), the entries they created
  (their authorship, D4). An `ACCOUNT` hold on the subject keeps the account and every entry,
  comment and attached file of theirs — UC-PRV-03 check 13's list, which is what "everything the
  person contributed" means in §4.1. The intake (matched by address, no entry, no author) is kept
  only by a `TENANT` hold, as the intake's sweep is (`RunRetention.go:690-705`). Several holds
  covering one row: the oldest in force is named (`ORDER BY placed_at, id`). One pure function in
  `core/domain/model/privacy` decides it; the erasure and the preview both call it — decided by
  worker; data-protection.md §4.1 rewritten in step 16.
- [x] D3 **What no hold reaches.** Credentials (tokens, calendar feeds, sync devices),
  notifications and unattached uploads go in every case — decided by `RunRetention.go:690-695`
  (a workspace-wide hold does not reach the notification history), data-retention.md §3 (sessions
  and devices swept without a hold), data-protection.md §5 (unreferenced media reconciled without
  one). The account's password and sessions stay with the kept account, so the person still signs in
  (check 13).
- [x] D4 **Anything kept keeps the account, and restricts it.** Kept contributions name their author
  through the account (§ 1); renaming or deleting it would anonymise held data, which check 10
  forbids. So when anything is kept, the account is not anonymised or deleted, its status becomes
  `RESTRICTED`, no pseudonym is assigned, and the case keeps its subject (`Perform.go:88-93` only
  clears it once the account is gone); the person's name stays on their unheld entries too until the
  remainder runs — the hold reaches the identity its kept data needs, and no further. Decided by
  worker. Rejected: re-attributing unheld contributions to a placeholder account (a second
  anonymisation mechanism rewriting other rows' authorship, beyond data-protection.md §4's "former
  user"); anonymising anyway (breaks check 10).
- [x] D5 **Partly completed is `COMPLETED` with what was kept.** New table `erasure_kept`
  (migration 0120, expand only): tenant, case (`ON DELETE CASCADE`), hold id (no foreign key: a
  restore rewrites `legal_hold` and must not take the record of what was kept with it), whether the
  account was kept, counts of entries, comments, assignments, intake, `recorded_at`, `erased_at`;
  primary key (tenant, case, hold); a partial index on (tenant, hold) where `erased_at IS NULL`;
  row-level security as every tenant table. The case answers `kept` (per hold: its id and scope, the
  counts, `erased_at` once the rest went) and `kept_legal_basis: ART_17_3_E` while it has rows; the
  status stays `COMPLETED`. Decided by worker. Rejected: a status `PARTLY_COMPLETED` — it changes
  data-protection.md §4's state machine, the `CHECK`, every `Closed()` reader and an enum clients
  switch on, and the remainder would have to move a closed case again (`Request.go:80-83`).
- [x] D6 **The release seeds the remainder.** `ReleaseLegalHold` gains a port its transaction calls
  (`RemainderSeeder`, declared in the lifecycle service, implemented in the privacy service, wired in
  `cmd/server/main.go`): one job `privacy.erasure_remainder` per case with a pending row for that
  hold, dedupe `dsr-remainder:<case>:<hold>`, in the release's own transaction — a committed release
  always has its jobs. The job (`presentation/worker/PrivacyErasureRemainder.go`, detached like
  `PrivacyRequest`) calls `ResumeErasure` in the privacy service, which locks the case row, runs the
  same erasure against the holds in force, replaces the case's rows (released holds' rows get
  `erased_at`, a hold still keeping takes the rest), assigns the pseudonym once the account goes,
  and writes `dsr.remainder_erased` (counts per place, the hold ids, no name). As the system actor,
  like `Perform` (`Perform.go:46-48`). Decided by worker. Rejected: one job per release that walks
  every case (one case's failure would hold up the others); an outbox event (no in-process consumer
  exists for it).
- [x] D7 **A hold and an erasure never pass each other.** `Active` takes
  `pg_advisory_xact_lock_shared(hashtext('legal_hold:' || current_tenant_id()))` before it reads;
  placing and releasing take the exclusive lock in their transaction. Every deletion path reads the
  holds inside the transaction that deletes, so a hold placed concurrently either waits for that
  transaction or is seen by it; readers never wait for each other. The erasure reads the holds in its
  main transaction. Decided by worker (known trap "read-then-write without a lock"; precedent
  `Audit.sql:13`).
- [x] D8 **A kept account's restriction cannot be lifted** while the case has a pending row:
  `RestrictProcessing` refuses `privacy.restriction_kept_by_erasure` (409); the web app offers no
  lifting today (UC-PRV-04 *Today*, check 1). Decided by worker: lifting it would make held data
  processable again, which check 10 forbids.
- [x] D9 **Every deletion path honours an `ACCOUNT` hold, and every held descendant.** `Target`
  gains `Contributors` — the accounts that created an entry, wrote a comment on it or uploaded a file
  attached to it, across the subtree a purge removes — read by one statement per batch, and only
  while an `ACCOUNT` hold is in force. `Blocking` matches an `ACCOUNT` hold against them. `Subtree`
  judges every entry it removes, not its root alone (the finding of § 1, fixed first, test first, in
  its own commit). Paths: `Purger.Subtree`, `Purger.Sweep`, `Sweeper.blocked`, the rule preview
  (`RetentionRules.go:393-402`). Decided by worker: LegalHold.go's own rule — a hold stored and not
  honoured is worse than one refused.
- [x] D10 **A hold names something here.** Placing a `CONTAINER`, `ITEM` or `ACCOUNT` hold reads its
  target under the caller's tenant and refuses `lifecycle.hold_target_not_found` (422, field
  `/scope_id`) when there is none; an anonymised account may be held. The check for the three old
  scopes is a finding, own commit before `ACCOUNT` is accepted. Decided by worker.
- [x] D11 **The preview.** Use case `PreviewErasure`, `GET
  /privacy/requests/{requestId}/erasure-preview?mode=`, read-only, `MANAGE_MEMBERS` and
  `privacy:read` as the list, `admin:tenants` for an `INSTALLATION` case; answers per hold what it
  would keep (scope, reason, counts, the account) for the mode asked, else the case's, else
  `ANONYMIZE`. Decided by worker: a read is a `GET` (api-guidelines.md §2); the retention preview's
  `POST …:preview` takes a body, this takes none. Rejected: answering it on every case in the list
  (one partition per case and page).
- [x] D12 **Codes.** New: `lifecycle.hold_target_not_found`, `privacy.not_an_erasure`,
  `privacy.restriction_kept_by_erasure`; `lifecycle.hold_account_scope_unavailable` leaves code and
  `locales` with its last use. Reused: `privacy.request_not_found`, `privacy.request_closed`,
  `privacy.installation_scope_denied`, `lifecycle.legal_hold`. `en` always; `de` for the `privacy.*`
  codes (the family is started, PH-02) and every `app.*` text; `lifecycle.*` stays English (no
  German entry exists). Decided by worker.
- [x] D13 **Personal data.** `erasure_kept` holds identifiers and counts, no name, no content: a new
  row in `docs/privacy/data-catalog.md` (`PERSONAL_TECHNICAL` as a reference, with the case's
  retention, deleted with the case); excluded from archives like `data_subject_request`
  (`core/application/archive/Record.go:651`). A kept account is processed only as stored and
  restricted. Decided by data-protection.md §3, §10.
- [x] D14 **The confirmation (UC-PRV-03 checks 1, 8, 11).** *Start* on an erasure opens a dialog that
  names the person and what the chosen mode removes, and lists what each hold will keep from the
  preview; it is offered only where the manifest grants `DELETE_CONTAINER` (`permissionVerdict`).
  The finding #1077 lands first in its own commit, the holds after it. Decided by worker
  (`docs/backlog/README.md`: a finding blocking a `Delivers` check is fixed in the task that found
  it).
- [x] D15 **The hold form (UC-LIF-06 checks 1, 8).** `ACCOUNT` picks a person from the workspace's
  people, `CONTAINER` a hub or collection from the tree, `ITEM` keeps its identifier field (side
  finding a); the list names the person or container; *Place* and *Release* appear only with
  `DELETE_CONTAINER`. Decided by worker (P-05, P-12).
- [x] D16 **#1203** — the privacy view moves to the generated type before this task extends it
  again; own commit. Decided by worker (apps/webapp/AGENTS.md).
- [x] D17 **Shared files.** PH-03, PH-04 and the other PH tasks may run beside this one; shared
  are `privacy.ts`, `PrivacyView.svelte`, the case output and `locales`. Whoever merges second
  rebases; the migration number is taken again before leaving draft. Decided by worker.
- [x] D18 **What this task does not do.** A restore that brings a hold back released does not seed
  its remainder (it runs on the next release of a hold it names, or not); a kept entry moved out of
  its hold's reach is erased by the next remainder run, not at the move. Both written into §4.1 as
  limits — decided by worker.

Open for the owner, but not in this task (D1): **O1**, the meaning of "every export but the hold's

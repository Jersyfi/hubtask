The work surface: one shape for everything people work on

The concept for Hubtask's work surfaces: everything to the right of the navigation in every product, from Today through lists, boards and entries to the service desk, pages, time and goals. Every object gets the same shape, every product keeps its colour, and every view does the same on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.

Building blocksWhat the design system gains

The product colours are already chosen (navigation.ts, one label token each). New is only that they also appear inside a product, through alias tokens product.<id>.surface and .ink in the semantic part of tokens.json, so that no component picks a colour itself.

Time (lime) and Goals (green) sit close together. That belongs to the navigation; the tile's glyph tells them apart, so no change is proposed.

ScreensEvery work screen, after the change

Pages, whiteboards and posts, the entry and the inventory, and search, create, files and the portal have chapters of their own below; here are the other screens and every product home on its own, with its empty state.

Pages in KnowledgeFind, trust, read, contribute

A wiki is used only when people find what they look for and can trust the page. People scan a page by its headings first; reading is the common act, writing the rarer one.

WhiteboardsCapture fast, arrange freely, moderate together, turn results into work

PostsNews that works like a page

Nothing of the kind exists today. A post has a date and an audience; it is read once, discussed, perhaps confirmed, and then steps back. Everything else works exactly as on pages.

The entryA normal entry and a service-desk ticket

Drawn with the story TAMA-18 filled to the edge (8 work packages, 10 activities of three types, 7 links, 9 attachments, 18 fields, 13 comments) and the ticket SA-12 with one SLA and two OLAs (six running deadlines), 5 affected items, 4 attachments, internal notes and replies.

InventoryOne name, one object, one place for upkeep

Query and searchOne query language for the whole platform

It builds on the line the search page already speaks and on the filter tree the server already checks and turns into parameterised SQL (rule 9, as api-guidelines.md §3.1 bounds it). Keywords stay English so that saved queries survive a change of language; suggestions and chips show the person's language.

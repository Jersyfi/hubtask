## The household

Set up through the product's own operations (`scripts/dev-workspace.sh`, then the API): *Paula
Parent* owns the workspace; *Max Parent* is invited as an administrator, *Lena Kind* as a member,
both redeeming their invitation from the caught mail. Paula made *Familie* (shared) and *Geschenke*
(private); Max made *Werkstatt* private and shared it again; Lena made *Tagebuch* (private) with a
collection *Sommer* in it — Lena holds no right to create hubs, so this is check 1's "whatever
their role".

## What was pressed

*Create hub* on the overview (dialog closed with Escape, nothing sent); on *Werkstatt* the menu's
*Make private* (confirmation closed with Escape, nothing sent); on *Tagebuch* the menu. Making a hub
private and sharing it again, end to end, are `privatehubs.test.mjs` and the integration tests.

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen, in either language.
- Nobody but a hub's own members sees it, its name, or its faces; the administrators' list names
  owners and sizes only.

## What was found, and fixed on the branch

- The faces, the members dialog and the assignee choice of a private hub listed the workspace's
  roles — people who reach nothing there. The people store now leaves the workspace's memberships
  out along a private hub (`scopesInForce`, `people.test.ts`); it also put *Import* behind Lena's
  workspace role instead of her ownership of the hub.
- The German texts first said *Workspace* where the application says *Arbeitsbereich*.

**When:** 2026-10-23 · **Commit:** the design system step on `ux-1690-menu-fits`
**Kind:** looking ([README](./README.md), "How to walk"): `pnpm --filter @hubtask/webapp build`, then a
temporary Playwright probe (Chromium) over the shared fixture's collection
(`apps/webapp/e2e/fixture.mjs`, `COLLECTION`), and the workbench's Menu case *At the foot of a
phone's screen* (`?story=wave-1-overlays-menu--foot&width=compact`). The probe was deleted
afterwards. The behaviour is proven by `apps/webapp/e2e/entrymenu.test.mjs`, "375 × 812 — the menu
of the last row on screen stays on screen, every item reachable", which fails on the code before
the fix (the menu ran from 582 to 1079 in a viewport 812 high); this shows it.

The screenshots are in [`F-1690-2026-10-23/`](./F-1690-2026-10-23/).

## Why the menu ignored the earlier try

Two causes, both measured in Chromium 156:

1. **The menu is wider than the room on either side of its ⋯.** The entry menu is as wide as its
   longest item; the ⋯ sits in the middle of the row on a phone. Every position that keeps the menu
   on one side of the ⋯ overflowed sideways, so no position fitted — whatever the block size did —
   and the engine kept the first one: below, past the foot. A plain popover with short items fitted
   sideways, which is why the earlier probe flipped and the menu did not.
2. **Chromium tries no more than five fallbacks.** With the inline flips added to the shrunk option
   the list had seven, and a fitting sixth or seventh was never reached.

The last resort in `packages/design-system/src/anchor.ts` now spans the whole width centred on the
trigger, takes `stretch` of the room on its side, and has a minimum of half of what the trigger
leaves of the viewport, so only the larger side holds it. `position-try-order: most-block-size` is
not used: it sorts the overlay's own position with the fallbacks and would open every short menu
upwards whenever there is more room above.

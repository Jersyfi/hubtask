## Found

The first pass of these screenshots showed *What counts* on *Shed* with an empty choice: it started
at the progress the hub set, which a one-level collection does not offer. Fixed on the branch
(`fix(webapp): a clipped progress starts the control at what is shown`); the walk now asserts the
starting choice, and the screenshot above is the fixed state.

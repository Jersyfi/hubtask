## Findings

* `SaveBar` (UX-03): in `profile-save-bar-375.png` the bar is a few pixels taller than the room its
  anchor adds (`--layout-bottombar-height`) and covers the foot of the last hint while it shows. The
  bar's padding around a full-size button exceeds the token. Reported to the coordinator; not this
  task's component.
* The entry page draws an empty *Details* card where nothing is set and nothing may be written (an
  archived entry, a viewer). It was so before this task for viewers; noted, not changed.

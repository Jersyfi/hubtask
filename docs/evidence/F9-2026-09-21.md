**Method.** Every stage is what a person would do on the screen, at each width in turn, with the
locators the F9-06 to F9-09 walks use, against the real server rather than the stubbed workspace
those walks use. Where the walk needed something the screen does not offer — the world itself —
the API did it under the same session. Everything the walk found is an issue (F6-15's rule);
the four that stood between the walk and a truthful "walked" were fixed in a pull request of
their own the same evening (#884), and the numbers below are from the pass on those fixes.

## 1. Sign in, the workspace, the frame

The sign-in form at 375 px, typed. After it the workspace: the bar with ☰ and the wordmark, the
banner, the sync line, the heading *Workspace* and the hub, and the bottom bar *Workspace ·
Search · Jumble · You* with *Workspace* current. `data-density="spacious"` on the frame, and the
controls are 48 px. ☰ opens the drawer with the tree alone — *House*, *Trash* — and *Create hub*;
none of the three destinations the bar already has is in it. *You* opens the account group as a
sheet from the bottom: *Walker* with the address at its head, then *Your settings · This
installation · Administration · Take the tour again · Sign out*.

At 768 px the bar holds ☰, the wordmark and the avatar with the name; the drawer holds both
groups in one tree — *Workspace · Search · Jumble · House · Trash*; no bottom bar, and the frame is
not spacious (a mouse). At 1440 px the navigation is pinned with the same five rows, the rail
toggle folds it to its marks and back without losing a row, and the avatar opens the same five
account items as the phone's sheet.

**Finding 6.** The workspace page itself had no `PageHeader`: *Create hub* was in the tree only,
which on a phone is behind ☰. The frame inventory's row said "and on the workspace page as the

The board: three columns and *No column* on the desk and the tablet; on the phone the strip *To

## 3. The entry three levels deep

`/items/{the tiles}` at every width. The trail *House › Kitchen › Order the tiles…* (the
parent alone on the phone); the checkbox, the title and the notes as text; the chips; the
section *Work package · 0/2* with *Tiles for the floor* open over its two activities (one done,
*1/2* on the branch) and *Tiles for the splashback*; *Close every level* closes both branches.

*Add Activity inside Tiles for the splashback* opens the form under that row, and *Create* puts a
third-level activity there — from the entry's page, which the old page could not. The title
edited in place and saved with Enter: `PATCH` with the title alone, the server's copy re-read
with it, and on the phone the bar's title follows. *Date* opened from its row — a popover on the
desk and the tablet, a drawer on the phone — the date typed, and the row reads *Oct 2, 2026*
with the chip beside the title; the server holds `2026-10-01T22:00:00Z` for Berlin. The checkbox
completes the entry from its page and reopens it. *History* as a tab.

**Finding 2.** The chips and the *Labels* row stood empty over an entry that carried *Materials*:
the page read `/items/{id}` without `expand=labels`, and without it the server answers no
`label_ids`. Fixed in #884 (#875).

**Finding 3.** Choosing *Walker* in the assignee panel answered *The request contains invalid
values*: the client sent `assignee_id`, the contract's `Assignment` says `account_id`, and the
server refused the other name as unknown — no assignment from the entry page had ever reached
it. Fixed in #884 (#876), the body typed from the contract.

**Finding 4.** After two writes on the entry the page said *Too many requests for this
credential*: every write causes 21 requests, and every `/items…` resource is re-read twice.
The walk raised the burst to 600 to proceed. Open as #877, with the counts.

**Finding 5.** `POST /items` with `label_ids` — a field `WorkItemCreate` declares — is refused as
unknown; the seed put the labels on afterwards. Open as #878.

**Finding 7.** Every load of the entry and every write on it logs a console error: the recurrence
`404` that means "no series". Open as #882.

## The integration environment

`main` deploys to `integration` on every push (`deploy.yml`). While the walk ran, the
environment served `main` with F9-06 to F9-08 on it (`/meta/capabilities`: `main-64c8dfa4`), and
its signed-out frame — the bar named *Application bar*, the sign-in form under the one `h1`, the
footer, no sideways scroll — was read at the three widths; the signed-in walk there needs the
`demo` workspace's password, which is the owner's
(`deploy/integration/README.md`) and not this session's, and is left to him or to a session he
gives it to. Nothing in the integration walk can find what the local one did not, except what
differs between a container on a laptop and a namespace on the cluster — which is the reason to
do it, and why it is written here as owed rather than done.

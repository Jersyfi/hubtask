# UX-19 — AI where its subject is

**When:** 2026-10-21 · **Branch:** `ux-19-ai-where-its-subject-is`
**Kind:** looking ([README](./README.md), "How to walk"). The web app was built from the branch and
walked by `apps/webapp/e2e/aiplaces.test.mjs` and `apps/webapp/e2e/aiask.test.mjs` in Chromium
through Playwright, English, at 1280, 768 and 375 px, light and dark, against the stubbed API of
`apps/webapp/e2e/fixture.mjs` with the AI reads routed in the walk. `EVIDENCE=<dir>` keeps the
screenshots; a choice of them is here.

**Use case checks it shows:** UC-AI-04 checks 6–9, as far as the screens go. What the server does
is proved against PostgreSQL as the application role: who may ask, list and decide along an entry's
and a collection's path (`test/integration/ai_suggestions_paths_test.go`,
`ai_container_paths_test.go`), the reading aids for a viewer, a viewer of one hub and a guest
(`ai_reading_aids_test.go`), duplicates of a title before it is an entry, a private hub and the
workspace next door included (`duplicates_by_title_test.go`, `duplicates_test.go`), the asker
watching and stopping a question and the fence a stopped question's answer meets (`ai_stop_test.go`),
and the budget's state read from the ledger (`ai_budget_state_test.go`).

## 2. The concept, read against the screen

Every row of ADR-0061 §1's table, at the five widths, on the real server. What holds:

* **One navigation, three drawings.** `compact`: the bottom bar with Workspace, Search, Jumble
  and You; the drawer from ☰ holds the tree only — the hub, the trash — and no destination is
  drawn twice; the bar reads ☰ · the page's title. `medium` (700): the drawer holds the primary
  group above the tree; the bar reads ☰ · wordmark · avatar. `expanded` and above: the side nav
  pinned, collapsing to a 56 px rail; the account menu with its five items, *Administration*
  among them for the owner. No search field in any bar. `data-density="spacious"` at 375 and
  nowhere above `medium`. One `h1` on every page at every width; no page wider than its viewport.
* **The container screen.** The head with *Add an entry* as a split button, *Filter and sort* as
  the secondary with its count, the menu in decision 5's order — act, set up, trash last; on a
  phone the secondary and the template list fold into the menu and the primary is pinned above
  the bottom bar. The three-way switch with *Show what is inside*, the star and the members
  beside it. The filter inline from `expanded` and a drawer below. The board one column with the
  columns as a strip at 375, scrolling inside itself at 700 and above, never widening the page.
  The bulk bar a sheet above the bottom bar.
* **The entry screen.** The trail through the levels; the head in place with the completion,
  the title, the chips; the whole subtree with *done of total* on every branch and the child
  type's name over it; the details rows saying *Add* where empty, opening a popover from `medium`
  and a drawer at 375, folded under the head there; comments and history as tabs.
* **The detail pane.** A row pressed at 1280 opens beside the list, the row stays current and
  focus stays in the list, Escape closes it; the same address at 1000 is a redirect to the entry.
* **The workbench.** The index in its groups behind ☰ at 375 and 768, the filter above it, the
  stories as tabs, the stage the document; no overflow at any width.

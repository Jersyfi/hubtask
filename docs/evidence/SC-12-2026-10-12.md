## The rotation, against the real server

Everything is in `rotation.log`.

1. **Start, ring `k1`.** Two workspaces, the offered model with its key, and Acme's own model with
   its key. `hubctl admin encryption show` counts `k1: 2, ai_provider=2`. The offered key is counted
   once, not once per workspace, and the installation's own level is counted alongside the
   workspaces.
2. **New key in front, ring `k2,k1`.** `k1` becomes the predecessor, still holding two values.
3. **`hubctl admin encryption reseal`** queues the two workspaces' rounds and the installation's own
   round. All three succeed. The installation's journal records `instance.secrets_resealed` with
   `rewrapped: 1, skipped: 0, active_key_id: k2` — counts and the key's name, nothing else.
4. **The census after the rounds:** `k1: 0`, and `k2: 2`. The offered model's key moved with the
   rest — this is the task's acceptance against a real server. With an installation sign-in
   provider present as well, the same drill is
   `TestARotationCompletesWithTheInstallationsOwnSecretsPresent`: a provider needs OIDC discovery
   against a real issuer, which the walk has none of.
5. **The current key removed, ring `k1` alone.** `show` marks `k2` `NOT IN RING` with 2 values,
   `ai_provider=2`.
6. **The operator's health report** answers
   `crypto.key_missing {key: k2, store: ai_provider, count: 2, counted_at: …}`. This is UC-INS-16
   check 3: the report says which kind would become unreadable, and when that was counted.
7. **The ring put back** (`k2,k1`). Everything is as it was in step 4.

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen, at either width.
- Each allowed source's use and cap side by side. The offered model's budget is the installation's

- No value, key or plaintext appeared in any answer, log or journal entry.

## What was found, and fixed on the branch

- **The operator's AI budget for one workspace was never stored.** The per-workspace quota write
  accepted `ai_tokens_per_day`, recorded the change in the trail and then dropped it. This was not
  seen by the walk; it was found while reading the write for this task. Fixed in `2c6caa28`, test
  first, with a test that holds every quota an operator may name to being stored.
- The hint under the budget still said "how many tokens a day this workspace may spend …, set by
  the installation", which is no longer true of the own model. It now names the offered model.

## What was not walked

- The workspace's day across midnight, a DST change and a zone changed mid-day: these are the table

# UX-13 — a looking walk: one palette for everything

**When:** 2026-10-21 · **Branch:** `ux-13-one-palette`
**Kind:** looking ([README](./README.md), "How to walk"). Chromium through Playwright. The built
web app over the walks' fixture (`apps/webapp/e2e/fixture.mjs`) at 375 × 800, 768 × 800 and
1280 × 800, light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`), English, on the collection *Kitchen*.

**Use case checks it shows:** UC-WRK-27 checks 1–4, as a person sees them. What proves them is
`apps/webapp/e2e/palette.test.mjs`, the palette test in `engines.test.mjs` (three engines) and the
unit tests under `apps/webapp/src/lib/palette/`; this walk is what a person looks at. The
screenshots are in [`UX-13-2026-10-21/`](./UX-13-2026-10-21/).

## What was walked

On each width and mode: `Ctrl K` with focus on the page, then the words "kitch", then `Escape`. At
1280 px also `?`.

| Picture | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `palette-here-<width>-<mode>.png` | Nothing typed: the actions of this page as its head offers them (*Add an entry*, *From a template…*, *Filter*, *Select entries*, the menu, *Show as …*), then *Open search*. From `medium` up under the bar's field; at 375 px in a dialog |
| `palette-kitch-<width>-<mode>.png` | "kitch": *Kitchen* (collection), *Search in Kitchen*, *Open Kitchen as a list / board / timeline*, *New task in Kitchen*, each with its kind; *All results* with the `Enter` it answers, *All results, with the filter open* |
| `help-1280-<mode>.png` | *Help and shortcuts*: every shortcut in four groups, both `⌘K` and `Ctrl K`, and *Take the tour again* |

What the script read on every width and mode: nothing thrown, no sideways scroll.

## What was found

Nothing that needs an issue. The fixture reports no `is_completed` query field, so the quick
narrowings that need it are not offered here, as on `main`; `e2e/search.test.mjs` walks them with a
manifest that reports it.

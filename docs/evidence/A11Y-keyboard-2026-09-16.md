## What was found

1. **No skip link, eleven stops to the content** (2.4.1). `AppFrame` now begins with one, hidden
   until it takes focus, landing on `<main id="main" tabindex="-1">`. Handled in the frame rather
   than left to the anchor, because the router takes every same-origin link and would have
   navigated to the same path with the fragment dropped.
2. **Every write from a list dropped focus to `body`** (2.4.3). The engine published `loading`
   over a `ready` state on every reload, so `EntryList` rendered its skeleton for the length of
   the reload and the focused row was destroyed. `SyncEngine` keeps a `ready` state until the new
   answer lands; `loading` is for the first read and for a retry after a failure. One rule in the
   engine rather than a guard in thirty-four consumers.
3. **Five inline editors left focus on `body` on both journeys** (2.4.3): the entry's edit form,
   the rename field, the add-an-entry form, the reminder editor, the recurrence editor.
   `focusFirst()` in the design system — an attachment that moves focus into what appeared and
   back to what opened it, or to `returnTo` where the opener was replaced.
4. **A card moved to another column lost focus** (2.4.3). `Board` remembers the card and focuses
   its menu button again once the board that arrives holds it.
5. **`UploadField`'s transparent input was a tab stop with a transparent ring** (2.4.7). Out of the
   tab order; the button beside it is the keyboard's way in; rule 5's ring moved to the target.
6. **The not-found screen had no heading** (1.3.1, 2.4.6). It has one.
7. **No test held the frame to one `<main>` and each screen to one `<h1>`.** `residue.test.js`
   renders every screen and counts; the frame is read where it is written.

Not a finding, recorded: `Select` and `Input` draw the ring on their shell through
`:has(:focus-visible)`, so a check that reads the focused element's own outline reports none —
the walk read the shell. And `Filter and sort` is a disclosure, not an overlay; `Escape` has no
job there.

Seen in passing, filed rather than fixed here: six design-system components paint a background
with `--bg-surface-raised`, a token `tokens.json` does not define (the hover of `ListRow`, the
selected row of `SideNav`, and four others) — an F5-12 or a fix of its own, not a keyboard matter.

## What this does not show

- A screen reader. The live region's text was read by a `MutationObserver`; what NVDA or
  VoiceOver make of it is F5-13's walk.
- The dialogs' own tab rings. A modal `<dialog>` traps `Tab` by the platform; the walk entered and
  left each one and read where focus landed, and did not walk inside.
- Firefox and WebKit. One engine.
- The routes' overlays that need data the fixture lacks: an entry's Move inside / Move out (one
  level), a rule's editor (no rule), a webhook's or a token's row actions.

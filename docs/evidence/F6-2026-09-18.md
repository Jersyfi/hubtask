## 3. Completed, retitled and moved while offline; the pending marks; the queue pushed; the server's word (F6-05, F6-06)

Offline: *Print the badges* ticked → done, *Waiting to be sent*; *Order lunch* moved up by its
row menu → above *Print the badges*, both rows marked pending; *Order lunch* retitled to *Order
lunch for twelve* on its screen; *Book the venue* retitled on its screen. The proxy started again:

```
POST /sync:push × 1
rows afterwards: "Book the venue, the big one" done, "Send the agenda" done, "Print the badges" done, "Order lunch for twelve" open — no row pending
the server: Book the venue, the big one | a0 | v3 · Send the agenda | a1 · Print the badges | a2 · Order lunch for twelve | a3 | v2
the line: "Connected"
```

Three of the four writes landed. **The move did not**: the queued `MOVE` carried `{order_key,
parent_id: null}` and no `collection_id`, and the push answered `REJECTED` /
`items.collection_or_parent_required`. The line showed it — *Not accepted · Moved · Order lunch
for twelve · The server did not accept this change. What you wrote is kept here until you dismiss
it.* — which is F6-06 doing what §9.5 asks; the change itself is wrong → **#777**.

**Seen twice on the entry screen**: right after an offline retitle, the entry's own state went to
*This needs a connection* with *Try again*, and *Try again* drew it from the copy at once. The
copy held the entry (read back from IndexedDB: the new title, `pending: true`). The read that
follows a queued write lands on the writer transaction the replica reuses, after its last request
and before its `complete` — `TransactionInactiveError`, caught as "no store" → **#776**. The list
screen showed the same once, after an offline completion made after the device was forgotten (§5).

## 4. A notes conflict from `hubctl`'s side, resolved both ways (F6-05, F6-06)

Offline, *Order lunch for twelve*'s notes written to *Twelve people, two vegetarian, one vegan.*
(queued as one `ITEM_PATCH` with `title` and `notes`, each under its own clock). The other side —
the walker, `PATCH /items/{id}` with `If-Match` — wrote *Ten people; the caterer confirmed.* The
proxy back:

```
POST /sync:push → CONFLICT on notes
conflicts in the store: field notes · mine "Twelve people, two vegetarian, one vegan." · theirs "Ten people; the caterer confirmed." · preservedCommentId set
the entry: "Two versions of the notes — The notes were changed by somebody else while you were away. — Show both versions"; the notes shown are theirs
```

*Show both versions* opened the resolver: *Theirs, in place* / *Yours, from this device* / *Your
version, as a comment on the entry*, and *Keep theirs* — the strip went, the notes stayed theirs.
The same again with *Twelve people after all: two vegetarian, one vegan.* against the walker's *The
caterer says nine now.*: *Write mine again* → the entry at v5 with my text, on the screen and on
the server; the strip went.

**Observed, not a defect of F6:** the edit form sends the title beside the notes although only the
notes changed, so the queued patch stamps both fields with a fresh clock; a concurrent title on
the other side would lose to an untouched one. §4.2's "fields the caller did not touch are not in
the log" is the server's rule for a request; the form is where the client should keep it →
**#779**.

## 5. A device forgotten from another session (F6-07)

lost is gone with the device, which is what forgetting one is for; noted, not filed.

*Contributor* on the workspace, behind Paula's step-up); Lena signed in with the start password and
chose a password of her own, so she held an open session and a password nobody else knew.

## What was pressed and what the API answered

Recorded in [`walk.log`](./PH-07-2026-10-12/walk.log):

- Each renewal: Lena's session opened before it answered `401` on its next request, and her previous
  password `401 auth.sign_in_failed`; the new start password is four groups of four characters.
- The renewal was walked twice, once per width; Lena chose her own password after each.
- The mail (from the catcher): subject "Confirm your address for Hubtask", the body naming Lena's
  account, the link `…/confirm-address#token=…`, a day, and "ignore this message" for anybody who
  did not ask. Before the confirmation `GET /accounts/me` answered no address.
- After the confirmation: sign-in by `lena@example.org` and by `lena` both `201`; a new start
  password for her `409 accounts.not_managed`; *Forgot your password?* for the address `202`.
- No script error on any screen.

The link in the mail names the installation's base address (`HUBTASK_BASE_URL`), as the reset link
does; the walk opened it at the workspace's host, which is where a person reading the mail in a
multi-workspace installation arrives through the workspace's own address.

## Found

- The chips that hold a select were pills around two lines, a blob at every width; they are framed
  now (`AskDialog.svelte`, its own commit).
- The fixture's manifest declares three query fields, so a chip's condition on a field it does not
  name is not sent - the client's `filterOf` keeps to the manifest as it should. The walk answers the
  fields the server declares (`core/domain/model/view/Field.go`).

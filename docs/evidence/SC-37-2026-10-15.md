**When:** 2026-10-15 · **Branch:** `claude/sc-37-connect-signed-in`
**Kind:** looking ([README](./README.md), "How to walk"). The web app was built from the branch and

English, 1280 × 900, against a stubbed API. `EVIDENCE=docs/evidence/SC-37-2026-10-15` keeps the two

to `/auth/callback` with a code and the state; nobody signed in at a provider, and the server's
exchange was not reached.

## What was pressed

*Suspend* on the own workspace and on *Household*, each dialog left with *Keep it running*; *Delete*
on the own workspace, the dialog left open for the picture. Nothing was sent. The second press that
suspends, and the cancel, are pressed by `apps/webapp/e2e/instanceworkspaces.test.mjs`, which reads
what the screen sent.

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen; no horizontal overflow of the page at either width.
- The walk's fixture lists four workspaces in single mode, which no single-mode installation has;
  the pictures show which controls are absent there, not a real single-mode listing.

## What was found

- At 375 px the workspace table still scrolls sideways inside its panel (noted in PH-10's walk; older
  than this task).
- The listing never answered `legal_hold` over REST: the use case filled it and the contract declared
  it, the REST mapping dropped it, so the console never saw a hold. Fixed on this branch in its own
  commit, test first (`TestTheInstallationListingAnswersTheLegalHold`).

## The tab icon's dark form, per engine

The icon's light colours are attributes, the dark ones one media rule inside the file. Probed
under the product's content security policy on the icon's own answer:

* **Chromium:** light and dark, following the page's emulated scheme (the engine walk asserts it).
* **Gecko:** light, and dark with the system set to dark (`ui.systemUsesDarkTheme`); the file's
  style applies under `style-src 'self'`.
* **WebKit:** the file's style applies under the policy (a probe without the media query drew the
  dark colours); its emulation does not reach an image's scheme, so the dark form was not seen
  switching there. An engine that ignored the rule would draw the light form.

No raster icon ships: a WebKit browser without vector tab icons shows its default, as before.

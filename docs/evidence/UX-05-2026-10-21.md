## Findings

* **A save bar shown over a field.** `installation-defaults-375.png` shows `SaveBar` covering the
  foot of the field just typed into, right after the change. UX-04's walk found the same: the bar is
  taller than the room its anchor adds. This is UX-03's component and is already reported by UX-04,
  so it is not changed here.
* **A wide page on a phone, fixed.** On `/administration/permissions` the role matrix made the whole
  page scroll sideways on a phone, because a cell's visually hidden label escaped the table's scroll
  box. The check-7 walk found it, and it is fixed in this branch (`fix(design-system): a table's
  hidden labels stay inside its scroll box`).
* **A plan's save keeps its own button.** It asks how many workspaces the change reaches before it
  is made, and a save bar would bypass that. This is named in the pull request.

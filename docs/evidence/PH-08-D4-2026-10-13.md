## What was pressed

*Place it*, *Lift it* twice; *Delete for good* and its confirmation; *Empty the trash* (not
confirmed); *Record it* twice, *How much to remove*, *Start answering it*, *Erase*; *Extend the
deadline*, *Extend it*; Kim's switch; the operator's proof, *Change these values*, *Save* twice; *AI
is part of everybody's work*, the basis, *Save*; *Understood*. The summaries were asked through the
API (`POST /items/{id}:summarize-thread`) as the owner, the stub's log read before and after.

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen; nothing scrolls sideways at 375 px.
- The stub received one prompt per summary that was accepted and none for the refused one.

## What was found, and fixed on the branch

- The trash said "a legal hold is in force on the **CONTAINER**": the refusal's message knew only
  the workspace's scope. Every scope now has its words, and a test holds each to them
  (`problem.test.ts`).
- The extension form started *The day you told them* on the device's calendar while the server
  bounds it by today in the workspace's zone: walked after midnight in Berlin against a workspace on
  UTC, the form's own default was refused. It starts on the workspace's today now (engine test in
  `privacy.test.mjs`).
- That refusal also put "{from} and {to}" beside the field: the field error carried no values. It
  does now (`Extension_test.go`).

- A refused read inside the suggestion list loses its audit entry, found by the tests of this

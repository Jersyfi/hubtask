## What was pressed

On the restore screen as the owner, *How* was set to *Replace this workspace*. Nothing was sent;
the rehearsal and the real run are covered by the tests (`restore.test.mjs`, `Hold_test.go`,
`restore_hold_test.go`).

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen; no horizontal overflow of the page at 375 px.
- None of the rows or sentences names which hold, on what or why — only that one stands.

## What was found

- At 375 px the workspace table scrolls sideways inside its panel, so the row's actions sit at the
  right edge; older than this task, and the same for every row.
- The restore screen's typed-name label reads "Type the workspace's name — — to confirm" in the
  walk: the fixture does not answer `GET /tenant`, so the name is empty. A fixture gap, not the
  screen's.
- The hold's warning stands above the red cost banner of the replace; the owner may prefer it
  below, or the cost banner left out while the replace cannot run.

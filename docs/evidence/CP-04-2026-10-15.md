## What it does not show

The behaviour behind the screen is the server's and is proved there, not here: the compilation and
the permission at the scope by `WebhookNarrowing_test.go`, the delivery by scope and condition and
the held-back row by `FanOutNarrowing_test.go`, the stored scope and the `FILTER_FAILED` row against
PostgreSQL by `test/integration/webhook_scope_test.go`.

## Seen and changed

The expression's sentences are shared by rules, retention rules and webhook conditions
(`expression.*`). Two of them named a rule or an entry - "a name this rule cannot see", "a field the
entry does not have" - and read wrongly under a webhook's condition. They now say "a name that is
not available here" and "a field the value does not have"; the field's hint names what is available.

## The installation

The operator offered *Large (EU)* — processor *Example AI SAS*, in the EEA, a key — through
`POST /admin/ai-providers`, and set the `ai` area through `PUT /admin/settings`: first *Only the
offered model*, locked (a provider for consumers); then *The offered model or their own* with *Their
own model must process content in the EEA*, locked (a provider for companies). *Anna* is a
consumer's workspace, *Acme GmbH* a company's.

## What was pressed

Anna's *Use Large (EU)* (once per width, her choice reset to no AI before each); on Acme's own
model, the kind, the address, a model and *In the European Economic Area* — not saved; the
operator's *Offer a model* (dialog left open for the picture, nothing sent). Offering, changing and
withdrawing a model end to end are `instanceai.test.mjs`, the registry tests and
`offered_ai_test.go`; choosing between sources is `aisettings.test.mjs` and `AiSource_test.go`.

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen, at either width.
- A workspace sees an offered model's name, processor and where, and nothing of its address, its
  models or its key.
- A source the rule does not allow is not shown: no own form under *Only the offered model*, no
  narrowing under a lock, no declaration looser than the minimum.

## What was found, and fixed on the branch

- **A workspace with no model of its own read the installation's offered model as its own** —
  address and models included — through `GET /ai-provider`: the one statement about the own row
  that did not name its workspace. Fixed in `222c7e0c` with the integration test that was missing
  (`TestAWorkspaceWithoutItsOwnModelReadsNoneWhileOneIsOffered`).
- **The own model's form offered a declaration below the minimum**, which the server refuses.
  Fixed in `9af3cd47`: only what the minimum admits is offered, and the engine test asserts it.

## What was not walked

- A suggestion actually answered by the offered model: no real AI provider is available to the
  project (milestone SC decision 9). That every AI feature runs through the same resolver with the
  offered model is `offered_ai_resolver_test.go` and the manifest test there.

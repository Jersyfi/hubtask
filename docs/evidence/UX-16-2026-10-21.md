## What was walked

| Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `1-list-menu` | A list row's ⋯: *Select* first, the entry's own actions, the moves with their reasons where a move is not possible now, then copy, share, link, archive, and the trash last |
| `2-board-menu` | A card's ⋯: the same items, with the board's columns after the moves; on compact the column the strip shows |
| `3-timeline-menu` | A timeline row's ⋯ at the end of its title: the same items without *Select* |
| `4-undo-message` | The trash from the menu: at once, and the frame's message with *Undo* |

## Found

* On compact a card's ⋯ sits near the start of the screen, and the menu, aligned to the trigger's
  end, shrank to the room beside it and broke every item into a column of words. Fixed in this
  pull request: the menu is as wide as its longest item, so it overflows and the engine flips it to
  the side with room (`packages/design-system/src/Menu.svelte`).
* On compact a long menu opened from a row in the lower half of the screen runs past its bottom
  edge instead of opening upwards; the items past the edge are reached by scrolling the page or by
  the keyboard (`4-undo-message` pressed the trash with End and Enter). Every menu has it; the
  entry menu is the longest. `finding` #1690.
* The labels dialog is modal, so the frame's message with *Undo* for a deleted label cannot be
  pressed while the dialog is open. The dialog's own list of deleted labels with *Bring back* is the
  way back there, and the message works once the dialog is closed (the readiness record, D8).

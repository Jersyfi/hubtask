## What was found, and fixed on the branch

* **The overview's links were the browser's default blue** — unreadable on the dark surface
  (`rgb(0, 0, 238)`). They take `--text-brand` now, as the product's other inline links do;
  `apps/webapp/e2e/adminoverview.test.mjs` compares their computed colour with the token's.
* **An auditor was told a group "gives a role you do not hold yourself"** — the server answers
  `members_changeable: false` to whoever may not manage members, and the screen read that as the
  role reason. Without `MANAGE_MEMBERS` the page's read-only notice is the one reason given;
  `apps/webapp/e2e/admingates.test.mjs`.
* **The fixture answered the operator register as a page envelope**, so the installation
  overview drew `{count}` in every walk, and answered no installation overview, so its three counts
  were blank. `/admin/operators` is a bare array in the fixture now, and `/admin/overview` answers
  `INSTANCE_OVERVIEW`. A real server answers both as the contract says; no product change.

* A degraded feature is named by its key (`ai: Unavailable`), as the bar's notice names it: the

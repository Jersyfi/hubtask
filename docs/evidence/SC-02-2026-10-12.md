## What was seen

- No script error on any screen, at either width.
- Google offers *Only people invited here* alone (check 2 as it reads today; whether Google may be
  admitted by Workspace domain is decision #1284).
- At 375 px the redirect address wraps inside its box and is shown whole (`redirect-box` in the log:
  its scroll width equals its width).

## What was found, and fixed on the branch

- The redirect address first stood in a read-only field, which cut it off at 375 px. It is text that
  wraps now, and the engine test measures it.
- The generic provider's instructions still said Hubtask would not connect an existing account and
  asked for email domains instead; since connecting asks for the account's own password, the
  sentence says that.

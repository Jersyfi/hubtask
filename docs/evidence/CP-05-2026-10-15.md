## What it does not show

The rest is proven by tests rather than this walk:

- **The fresh proof for adding and removing** is the Playwright walk in `instancetargets.test.mjs`: refused, proven, sent again with the proof.
- **The boundary** is `test/integration/installation_backup_target_test.go`: a workspace session reaches no installation target, method by method, and the installation reaches no workspace's target.
- **The export itself** is `test/integration/export_installation_target_test.go`: a workspace is exported to an installation target as the application role. The workspace's list stays empty, and the tenant-only opener finds nothing.
- **The service rules** are `core/application/service/admin/InstanceBackupTargets_test.go`: the scope and the register, the step-up, and no credential in the answer or the journal.

## Seen and left

- **Encryption field.** The form offers *Encryption* for an installation target as it does for a workspace's. The export ignores it: an export is always unencrypted (tenant-export.md, the table at its head). The field is kept because the target is the same aggregate, and the hint already says what encryption means for an archive.
- **Export dialog copy.** The old copy said the archive is encrypted and that the target may be left empty for a default. Both statements were wrong, and both are corrected here.

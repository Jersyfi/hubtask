# SC-10 — a using walk of terms of use, agreed and agreed again

**When:** 2026-10-12 · **Commit:** `8059a748` (branch `claude/sc-10-terms-of-use`)
**Kind:** using ([README](./README.md), "How to walk"). The server was built from that commit with
the web app embedded. It ran in single mode with **no `HUBTASK_SMTP_HOST`**, so an invitation
answered its link. Its database was its own PostgreSQL 16 (pgvector), migrated to 0140 from empty
and reached as `hubtask_app` (row level security in force). The installation was set up through
`POST /auth/installation:set-up` as the workspace "Our home" with its owner `anna@home.example`.
The browser was Chromium through Playwright, light theme, English, at 1280 × 900 and at
375 × 812 (a phone, touch).

**Use case checks it shows:**
- UC-ID-19 check 1 and UC-ID-07 check 4: the invitation is not accepted without the agreement; the
  account keeps the link, the version and the time.
- UC-ID-19 check 2: after the version changed, the next sign-in meets the agreement step.
- UC-ID-19 check 3: declining opens no session, and the card says whom to ask.
- UC-ID-19 check 4: the trail records each agreement with the version and the link.
- UC-ID-07 check 1: the invitation card names the workspace and the invited address.

Check 5 (no terms, no step and no box) and check 6 (the step after a provider's return) are not in
this walk: check 5 is the state before step 1 of it and is proven by the engine walk
`apps/webapp/e2e/terms.test.mjs` and the registry tests, and no real identity provider is
available to the project (milestone SC, decision 9) - check 6 is proven by
`core/application/service/identity/TermsSites_test.go` for every kind of arrival.

The screenshots are in [`SC-10-2026-10-12/`](./SC-10-2026-10-12/).

## What was pressed

- On *Sign-in* settings: the terms link and the version, *Save*, and the step-up's password.
- Sign in as the owner twice (375 px, then 1280 px); *Agree and continue* once.
- The invitation was created through `POST /accounts:invite` as the owner (no mail, so the answer
  carried the link); the link opened in a browser of its own, twice (375 px only looked).
- *Set the password and sign in* without the box, then with it.
- Version 4 was set through `PATCH /tenant` with a step-up, as the owner; the owner's own sign-in for
  it met version 3, which she had agreed to, and no step.
- Sign in as Bert three times: *Decline* at 1280 px and at 375 px, then *Agree and continue*.

## What was seen

- **The database after the walk:**
  - `account`: Anna holds `https://home.example/terms`, version `3`, with a time; Bert the same link
    with version `4`, with a time.
  - The trail holds three `auth.terms_agreed` entries, each with `terms_url`, `terms_version` and
    `terms_set_by` `WORKSPACE`: Anna's version 3, Bert's version 3 in the same moment as his
    `auth.invitation_redeemed`, and Bert's version 4. None carries any text of the terms.
- **Declining sent nothing**: the agreement route was called only for the agreements above.
- **No script error** on any screen.
- **The invitation card names the workspace before anything is typed.** The read goes as
  `POST /auth/invitations:inspect` with the token in the body, after the token has left the
  address bar.
- **The card's host line reads "to localhost:18490"**, the installation's base address, as in the
  SC-13 walk.

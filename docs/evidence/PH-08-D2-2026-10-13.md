## The household

Set up through the product's own operations: the installation's setup
(`scripts/dev-workspace.sh --bootstrap`) made *Paula* the owner of *The Winters*; Paula invited
*Max Winter* as an administrator and *Tim Winter* (the teenager) as a member, each redeeming the
invitation from the caught mail; Paula made the hub *Family* with *Groceries*. *Gerda Winter*, the
grandmother, has no mail address — she is made in the walk.

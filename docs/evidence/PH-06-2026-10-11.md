## What was pressed

*Add* on *People* (one `POST /accounts:create-managed`); *Sign in* with the sign-in name in capitals
and the start password; *Set the password and sign in* with a new password. Both walks ended signed
in as the managed person; `GET /accounts/me` answered the folded sign-in name and `ACTIVE`.

## What was seen

- The start password is four groups of four characters; it appeared nowhere but the one-time panel.
- A first attempt chose a new password containing "password": refused by the common-password rule,
  the card kept the step - the rule as it is for everybody.
- No script error on any screen.

## What was found, and fixed in this pull request

- The one-time panel used the access token's words ("Show token", "Token zeigen"): the start password
  now has its own labels.
- The change step after a sign-in name said the password "no longer meets this workspace's rules",
  untrue for a start password: it now asks for a password of one's own.
- The German texts said "Workspace" where the product says "Arbeitsbereich".

- The managed-accounts switch locked by the installation: shown by `apps/webapp/e2e/managed.test.mjs`
  over the fixture, not walked against a server.

## A second walk: the password switched off (O1, decision #1268)

**When:** 2026-10-11 · **Commit:** `5210cc74` (same branch) · **Kind:** using, as above - the
server built from that commit with the web app embedded, its own PostgreSQL 16 (pgvector) migrated
to 0127, a workspace `home` made by `scripts/dev-workspace.sh`, two managed accounts (`lena`,
`oma.erika`) made by the owner through `POST /accounts:create-managed`, Chromium at 1280 × 900 with
the browser's language English, then German.

**One step outside the product's operations:** the workspace's provider *Family ID* was written
into `identity_provider` with SQL - configuring one through the product runs discovery against a
real issuer, which a walk on one machine does not have. Nobody pressed its button. The password was
then switched off by the owner through `PATCH /tenant` (`sign_in_policy.methods: [OIDC]`) behind a
step-up; `/auth/sign-in-rules` answered `methods: [OIDC]`, `sign_in_names: true` and the provider.

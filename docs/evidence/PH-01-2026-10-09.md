## What was pressed

At both widths: the erasure row's *Start answering it* was focused and **Enter** pressed — the
dialog opened with focus on its close control (*Keep the case open*); **Escape** closed it and
nothing was sent. The confirmation without a hold was opened by a click. On the retention screen
the scope select was set to *One person's data*, then to *A hub or a collection*. No write was
confirmed.

## What was seen

- No script error on any screen, and no horizontal overflow on the retention screen at 375 px.
- Focus lands in the dialog and returns on Escape; the dialog's footer holds *Keep the case open*
  and *Erase*, the latter in the danger tone.

## What was found

- **Fixed during the walk:** a hold's line read "their name on 0 entries"; a zero is now said in
  words ("no entry"; `4e40bf49`).
- The per-hold lines in the dialog are set smaller than the paragraph above them; readable at both
  widths, but the owner may prefer one size.
- The confirmation's copy uses " - " as a dash where some of the screen's older copy uses "—".
- The full-page screenshots at 375 px show the fixed bottom navigation in the middle of the page:
  an artefact of the full-page capture, not of the screen.
- A completed case still says *Owed …* under its deadline; that line is older than this task.

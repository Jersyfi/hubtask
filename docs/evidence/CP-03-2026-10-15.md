# CP-03 — a looking walk of recording a request with the day it arrived

**When:** 2026-10-15 · **Commit:** `9fb98c341` (branch `claude/cp-03-received-on`)
**Kind:** looking ([README](./README.md), "How to walk"). The built web app ran over the walks'
fixture (`apps/webapp/e2e/fixture.mjs`) in Chromium through Playwright, in the light theme and
English, at 1280 × 900 and 375 × 812. The workspace counts in Europe/Berlin and the clock was fixed
at 2026-10-15 09:00 UTC. The recording's answer was routed as the server gives it: received
14 August, owed to the end of 14 September in Berlin. That the server answers so is proven by
`TestTheDeadlineIsOneCalendarMonthFromTheDayOfReceipt` and `TestTheDayOfReceiptIsTheWorkspaces`.
This walk is what the owner looks at.

**Use case check it shows:** UC-PRV-01 check 2.

The screenshots are in [`CP-03-2026-10-15/`](./CP-03-2026-10-15/). What the script read on each
screen is in [`walk.log`](./CP-03-2026-10-15/walk.log).

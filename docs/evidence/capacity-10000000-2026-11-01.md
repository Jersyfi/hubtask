## What the run found - open

None of these is fixed yet, and none is explained: the dataset is torn down with the run, so each
plan has to be read on a new seed (`EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)`, about 30 minutes to seed).

1. **A collection's page in the middle and smallest workspaces** is 345 to 561 ms at P95 against
   8 ms at 10⁶ and 11 ms in the largest workspace at 10⁷ - not a growth with entries but a
   different plan, likely a switch away from `wi_level_order_idx` on these collections' statistics.
2. **The board and the count over the hub in the largest workspace** grow with its entries (about
   1 s at 1.2 million) - over the 200 ms read target; whether a kept count as the hub's level has
   is the answer is the next worker's to read from the plan.
3. **The hub's level in the largest workspace** is 349 ms at P95 with kept figures, against 7 ms at
   10⁶: the read is not answered from the kept counts alone at this size.
4. **1 965 PostgreSQL `canceling statement due to user request`**, which the 10⁶ runs did not have,
   and no server error beside them: statements whose request ended first - the harness's windows
   closing on slow paths, or the server's own deadline. Not yet told apart.
5. **R3's walk is not a walk yet**: each of the three workspaces' walks saw only 23 answers of 403
   - the load token carries `items:read`, `items:write`, `containers:read` and
   `automation:manage` only, so the web app's other reads are refused. The walk needs a token
   with every scope the app reads before its console count says anything about the product.

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 **The model never writes CEL** — decided by: worker; confirmed by the coordinator after
  the review. CEL is sandboxed and bounded (security.md §7 and §10.1, automation.md §1.2), so the
  danger is not escape but meaning: material a person did not write (a label or collection name, a
  quoted mail) can steer a model (T-10) into an expression whose effect differs from what anybody
  reads — `|| true` hidden in a gate, a `dedupe_key_expr` that collapses every run, a
  `body_template` that carries `item.notes` out. The composer reads back only its own shapes, so a
  free expression is drawn as opaque text. Rule 9 states the same principle for SQL, ai-first.md §2
  that a model picks from what it was shown. So: conditions are answered as **structured sentences**
  from the composer's vocabulary — `{mode: all|any|none, items: [{subject, op, a, b}]}`, one level,
  at most 10; subjects `type`, `title`, `notes`, `completed`, `archived`, `due`, `label`,
  `assignee`, `parent`, `depth`, `actor`, `hour` (not `bucket` or `field`: no set is shown for them).
  Values: `type` from the item-type enum; numbers are JSON integers, range-checked — `hour` 0–24 with
  `a < b`, `depth` 0–4, `days` 0–365; `label`, `assignee`, `actor` identifiers from the shown sets;
  text bounded to 200 characters. The producer compiles them with a Go twin of `compileNode` in
  `core/domain/model/automation` (literals escaped as the composer escapes them, RE2 metacharacters
  escaped), then once more through `expression.Compiler` (rule environment, Boolean); a failure
  drops the condition and names it. Branch conditions, `dedupe_key_expr` and `body_template` are
  never drafted. One golden vector file holds the two compilers byte for byte (§4).
- [x] D2 **An allowlist of entry-level action kinds, every field classified** — decided by: the
  coordinator after the review (B1). `draftableActions` sits beside `acceptance` in code; an action
  kind outside it is dropped and named (`automation.draft.kind_not_offered`), and so is one the
  asker's manifest does not serve. Each `rule: true` field is classified; a field of kind `id`,
  `id_list`, `object` or `any` that is not classified is dropped. `item_id` and `entry_id` are the
  run's (§2.2) and never drafted.

  | Kind | Shown reference | Closed enum | Bounded text | Never drafted |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `ADD_LABEL`, `REMOVE_LABEL` | `label_id` (labels) | — | — | — |
  | `ASSIGN_WORK_ITEM` | `account_id` (people shown) | — | — | — |
  | `UNASSIGN_WORK_ITEM`, `COMPLETE_WORK_ITEM`, `REOPEN_WORK_ITEM`, `CLEAR_DUE_DATE` | — | — | — | — |
  | `MOVE_WORK_ITEM` | `target_collection_id` (collections shown) | — | — | `target_parent_id`, `target_bucket_id`, `before_item_id`, `order_key` |
  | `CREATE_WORK_ITEM` | `collection_id` (collections), `assignee_id` (people) | `type` | `title`, `notes` | `parent_id`, `bucket_id`, `content_language`, `auto_assign`, `start_at`, `due_at`, `due_date_only`, `due_time_zone`, `calendar_uid`, `label_ids`, `custom_fields`, `cover`, `before_item_id`, `member_ids` |
  | `ADD_COMMENT` | — | — | `body` | `parent_comment_id` |
  | `CONVERT_JUMBLE_ENTRY` | `collection_id` (collections) | `type` | `title` | `bucket_id` |

  Text is bounded to 500 characters (titles to the domain's own limit, checked again at the run).
  Explicitly: **assigning** only to people shown; **moving** only to collections shown; **no
  notify-style kind** (UC-AUT-09's are not built, and telling people is not drafted); no outbound
  kind, no destructive kind, no asking kind (`DESCRIBE_RULE`, `AI_*`), no `BRANCH`; flow kinds
  `WAIT` (an ISO duration, `ValidActionShape`) and a final `STOP`. `test/architecture` holds the
  allowlist to the catalogue: every kind exists, every `rule: true` field of each is classified,
  none is `Destructive`. Triggers: `EVENT` with an event type from `event.Types()` and
  `changed_fields` from the closed set of item fields the event declares; `RELATIVE_DATE` through
  `ValidOffset`; `MANUAL`, `INBOUND_WEBHOOK`, `JUMBLE_ENTRY`. **`SCHEDULE` is not drafted** (an RRULE
  is a language of its own) and is named (`automation.draft.schedule_not_drafted`). `run_as`,
  `throttle`, `on_error` and the name are never drafted.
- [x] D3 **The shown sets are narrowed to what the words name** — decided by: worker; matching made
  script-aware after the review (S8). People, collections (with hubs) and labels are read through
  the ordinary listings as the asker: `ListMemberships` at each scope on the asked scope's path,
  each account resolved by `GetAccount`; `ListContainers`; `ListLabels` of the shown collections.
  A name is kept when, after case folding and NFC, the whole name is a substring of the
  description, or a word of the name of three or more letters is, or a word of the description of
  four or more letters begins a word of the name; for scripts written without spaces (Han, Thai,
  Japanese kana) the whole name of two or more characters as a substring. Order fixed: by name,
  then identifier; at most 20 per set; plus the asker as "you" and the asked scope's own container.
  A tested example per script: Latin, Cyrillic, Arabic, Devanagari, `zh-Hans`, `th`. Someone who
  holds a role only through a group is not listed by `ListMemberships` and so is never shown; an
  assignment to them is named as not drafted. Anyone the exclusion keeps out (objection or
  restriction) is never in the people set, and their parts of the description are scrubbed as
  everywhere. Reason: data minimisation, and a smaller closed set is a smaller injection surface.
- [x] D4 **The draft belongs to the person who asked: target type `ACCOUNT`** — decided by: worker,
  check made explicit after the review (B3). For target type `ACCOUNT`, listing, accepting and
  dismissing are allowed exactly when `actor.AccountID == target_id`, under the token scope
  `automation:manage`; otherwise `suggestions.not_found` (404), with no read of anything. The digest
  is the account's identifier; staleness does not apply, since the save re-validates everything
  through `CreateRule`, which asks `AUTOMATION` at the scope as today. Rejected: a `WORKSPACE`
  target filtered per row by the scope's permission — a second visibility rule beside the target's.
- [x] D5 **Describing asks what writing a rule asks** — decided by: automation.md §2.1 (1).
  `DescribeRule` authorises `AUTOMATION` at the asked scope's path, token scope
  `automation:manage`, `Entitlement: AutomationRules`, then AI availability. `Ask.queue` takes the
  access request as a parameter instead of fixing `WRITE_ITEMS`.
- [x] D6 **The scope travels as references** — decided by: the coordinator after the review (B2).
  The job payload carries `scope_type` and `scope_id`; the worker reads them back, and
  `Sources.Material` takes a `Question` (target, prompt, scope, the request's words) instead of
  four parameters. The answer may narrow the scope to a shown hub or collection in which the asker
  holds `AUTOMATION`; anything else keeps the asked scope.
- [x] D7 **What could not be drafted** — decided by: worker. `not_drafted` lists `{part}` — a
  fragment the model quoted, kept only where it occurs in the **original** description (case and
  whitespace folded) — and `{code, params}` for what the narrowing dropped
  (`automation.draft.kind_not_offered` {kind}, `…reference_not_shown` {field},
  `…field_not_drafted` {field}, `…branch_not_drafted`, `…schedule_not_drafted`,
  `…condition_not_expressible`, `…no_trigger`, `…no_action`). A draft with no trigger or no action
  is still recorded. `not_drafted` is a **told** key: shown, never merged into the applier's input;
  `applicablekeys_test` learns `toldKeys` beside `grown`.
- [x] D8 **Saving is accepting** — decided by: worker, from ai-first.md §2. The client loads the
  payload onto the canvas as an unsaved new rule; *Save* sends `AcceptSuggestion` with the canvas as
  `overrides`. Through REST and MCP, `overrides` must carry `name` and `run_as` (a draft holds
  neither; `CreateRule` refuses without them), and anything else it carries replaces the draft's —
  partial overrides are laid over the payload key by key, said in the descriptor and the contract.
  The acceptance table gains `{ACCOUNT, RULE}: {Applier: "CreateRule", NoTarget: true}`. The accept
  answer is `AcceptedSuggestion` with optional `created_id`, answered only by `:accept`.
  `Request.Apply` with `KindRule` is refused in `Produce`: an AI answer never becomes a rule without
  a person.
- [x] D9 **Bounds** — decided by: worker. At most 10 actions, one condition group of at most 10
  sentences, 2000 characters of description (the `ai_request` check).
- [x] D10 **Where *Describe it* lives** — decided by: worker. In the editor of a new rule: a ✦
  control on the empty canvas and in the head; the description in a small dialog; while the job

- [x] D12 **Message codes** — `en` always; `de` for `app.flow.*` (fully translated); the server

- [x] D13 **Erasure** — decided by: the coordinator after the review (S6). The erasure deletes

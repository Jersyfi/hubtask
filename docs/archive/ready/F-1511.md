## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 The three doors call `MayAdmit`. `notAdmitted` keeps only the invited account's pointer to
  its link, still under `MayAdmitInvitation`. `MayAdmitWithProof` stays as the reading behind
  `MayAdmitInvitation` and nothing else, and its comment says so. — decided by: owner, #1511 (b) →

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 A remover in the retention run, beside the jumble's: the catalogue entry gains
  `Actions: [HARD_DELETE]` and `Blockable: [legal_hold]`; a default policy of 30 days, no lower
  bound (the jumble's and the sessions' reason: a shorter period keeps less) — decided by: the task
  text, data-retention.md §3's period.
- [x] D2 Past the period every row goes, except a `PENDING` one a living job still names: the delete
  carries `NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM job j WHERE j.kind = 'webhook.deliver' AND j.state IN
  ('PENDING','RUNNING') AND j.tenant_id = d.tenant_id AND j.payload->>'delivery_id' = d.id::text)`
  for pending rows. Cheap enough: the batch is bounded, and the living jobs of one kind are few — the
  queue's backlog, which A-06 keeps small — decided by: worker, on the review. Rejected: keeping every
  pending row (a row whose job dead-lettered and was purged would stay for ever); deleting every
  pending row (a job would meet a missing row).
- [x] D3 Anchored on `created_at` (the catalogue entry's anchor): an attempt's age, not its event's —
  decided by: data-retention.md §3.
- [x] D4 data-retention.md §3's row and the data catalogue's row drop "not enforced yet"; §3 names the
  remover — decided by: worker; a statement of what is now true.

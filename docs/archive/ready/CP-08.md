## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 The leader removes them, installation-wide, in its housekeeping — decided by: the task
  text; the table has no tenant and nothing enumerates tenants.
- [x] D2 Once an hour, on the first tick of the hour (as `applyOperators` paces itself by the
  minute), at most 10,000 rows per run, oldest first: a backlog clears over hours without holding
  the leader's tick; a stateless pace, so a new leader keeps it — decided by: worker. Rejected:
  every tick (a scan every ten seconds for nothing), a job of its own (the queue removing its own
  rows while claiming them).
- [x] D3 All four terminal states go after seven days, dead letters included: the catalogue says
  "7 days after completion", and a dead letter is the queue's completion of the work. RB-A07 says
  so ("a dead letter is removed seven days after it was given up; requeue or copy it before") —
  decided by: worker. Not raised to the owner: it implements the catalogue's period; keeping dead
  letters longer would be the deviation.
- [x] D5 The removal is counted: `hubtask_job_purged_total{state}`, the closed set of the four
  terminal states, never a kind's payload or a tenant (rule 10, observability-reliability.md §3.2) —
  decided by: worker, on the review's condition.
- [x] D4 The period is the catalogue's seven days as a constant beside the purge, not a setting —
  decided by: worker; the catalogue names no setting, and data-retention.md §3's kinds are periods a
  workspace chooses, which a job is not.

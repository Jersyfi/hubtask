# UX-14 — Capture from anywhere

**Task:** UX-14 · issue #1646 · `docs/backlog/milestone-UX.md`
**Checked against:** `origin/main` at `c91016fbd` on 2026-10-21 (UX-09 and UX-13 merged); built on
UX-04's branch `origin/ux-04-explain-without-breaking` (#1682, the toast host) until it lands on
`main`, then rebased onto `main`. Read beside it: UX-12 `origin/ux-12-simple-list` (#1684, `amount`
and the line parser), UX-29 `origin/ux-29-honour-structure` (#1678, hidden values refused), UX-16
`origin/ux-16-one-entry-menu` (#1677, undo)
**Verdict:** ready

One line, typed anywhere, becomes an entry in the right place. `Q`, a *Capture* button in the bar
and the action in the phone's bottom bar open one capture field. While the person types, the field
shows what it recognised as chips: a date and a time, a person (`+`), a hub (`#`), a collection
(`@`), a label (`!`) and — once UX-12 lands — an amount. Which of these it recognises follows the
target collection's structure in force. Enter creates the entry and a toast names the place, with a
link and *Undo*.

The work is **client-only** on the server side of the contract. One `POST /items` already takes the
title, the due date with its zone and all-day flag, the assignee and the labels in one transaction
(`CreateWorkItem.go:78-115`), and the jumble's `POST /jumble/entries` takes a subject. The structure
in force already carries a `capture` setting that says what a capture field recognises
(`StructureSettings.go:93-108`, `api/openapi.yaml:15062-15069`). No contract, no migration, no
message code reaches the server. One design-system part is built: `BottomBar` learns an action
item (D2).

## 3. Decisions

- [x] D1 **Three ways in, one field, owned by the frame.** `Q` is a `SHORTCUTS` row and a `capture` handler in `AppFrame`'s `listenForShortcuts` call; the bar's `end` carries *＋ Capture* (`Button`, `plus`, `aria-keyshortcuts="Q"`) before the notice mark from `medium` up; on `compact` the bottom bar's action opens the field. All three call `capture.summon()`; one `CaptureDialog` is mounted by the frame. — decided by: worker — inventory §E0 places them; one owner so two ways in cannot open two fields.
- [x] D2 **`BottomBar` gets an action item, drawn as a button.** `navigation.ts` gains a `capture` destination with `target: {kind: 'action', action: 'capture'}` in the `places` band, so §11.3's "one list" holds; `BottomBar`'s `Destination` gains an optional `action` flag: an action is a `<button>` (no `href`, no `aria-current`), placed by its order in the list — after *Search*, before *Jumble*. With the jumble absent the bar holds four items and the ＋ is third, not centred; that is accepted.
